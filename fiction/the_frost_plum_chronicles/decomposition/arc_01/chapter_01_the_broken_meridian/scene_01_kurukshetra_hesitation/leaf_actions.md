My fingers gripped the brass cylinder. The metal was freezing cold.

Across the dirt, I saw the faces of the people I had sworn to protect, charging forward with bloodlust in their eyes. 

*Turn it,* my brother's voice echoed in my skull. *Do your duty!*

My thumb touched the firing latch. 

My breath caught. My hand trembled.

*I... I can't.*
