# Chapter 1: The Duty of the Blade

The sky above the battlefield was the color of bruised iron.

Across the plains of Kurukshetra, three million soldiers stood in iron ranks, their spears bristling like a forest of needles. But the sharpest blade twisting in my gut wasn’t the enemy's vanguard.

It was their damn flags.

Flapping in the hot, ash-choked wind were the crests of my own clan. My cousins. My childhood friends. Idiots I had shared wine with, disciples I had beaten black and blue with wooden training swords until they could split waterfalls—all standing under the black banners of the Great Evil.

They had swallowed the poison whole. Seduced by a fanatic who preached that humanity was an incurable cancer and that every thinking machine and artificial intelligence ever created had to be hunted down and wiped out to purify the earth, they marched to eradicate the world. They couldn't see that behind the zealous rhetoric about purging humans and smashing machines, a handful of greedy old bastards were pulling the strings, using forbidden super-weapons to clear the board for their own private empire.

*Smack!*

A heavy hand slammed into the center of my back, nearly knocking the breath from my lungs.

"Stop scowling like an old widow, Jin!"
