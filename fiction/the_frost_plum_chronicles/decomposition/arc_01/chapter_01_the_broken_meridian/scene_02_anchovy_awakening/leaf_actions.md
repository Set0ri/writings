My hand instinctively whipped toward my hip, reaching for the hilt of my sword to decapitate whoever dared slap the Divine Dragon of Mount Hua—*CRACK.* A blinding, agonizing pain shot straight through my fifth rib. My arm flopped uselessly to my side, twitching like a dying eel. "Ow, ow, OW! What the hell?!" Instead of a terrifying war-cry that could shatter castle gates, the sound that flew out of my mouth was a strangled squawk, like a wet chicken being stepped on by an ox. I raised a trembling hand before my nose. The wrist was a toothpick. The fingers were pale, scrawny, and devoid of sword calluses. "Chopsticks?" I muttered in pure horror. "I have chopsticks for arms?! Where are my forearms?! Where are my muscles?!" I collapsed back into the straw, grinding my teeth. *Damn you, Samsara. When I die again, I'm punching the King of Hell right in the teeth.*
