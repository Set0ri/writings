# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The Anchovy Awakening: Southfall Mud & Two Centuries
**Council Score:** Total 0.945 (Canon: 0.96, Kinetics: 0.94, Sensory: 0.95, Feral: 0.92)

---

*What is this?* I thought, my brain rattling inside my skull like a loose pebble in an iron kettle. *Did the Netherworld run out of budget? Where is the Sea of Fire? Where are the demons flaying my skin? Why does the afterlife smell like spoiled mackerel?!*

I raised a trembling hand to my face.

I froze.

I blinked once. Twice.

The hand in front of my nose was the size of a teacup. The wrist was a toothpick. The fingers were pale, scrawny, and completely devoid of sword calluses. 

"What... what is this?" I muttered in pure horror, turning the hand over. "Chopsticks? I have chopsticks for arms?! Where are my forearms?! Where are my muscles?!"

"Jin-ah! Oh, thank the ancestors, you're alive!"

A woman dropped to her knees beside my straw pallet, burying her wet face against my collarbone. She was thin, dressed in a faded hemp tunic with salt rings around the neck, her black hair held back with a carved bone pin.

Behind her stood a broad-shouldered man in a patched linen shirt, holding a wooden bowl of steaming broth. His knuckles were raw, his face lined with deep exhaustion, but the moment he saw my eyes open, his knees visibly buckled in relief.

"You reckless little bastard," the man choked out, half-laughing, half-sobbing. "Two days! The apothecary said you cracked your skull down to the brain-pan. What were you doing tumbling off the rocky bluff past the boundary fence?!"

I stared at the ceiling, my mouth opening and closing like a landed carp.

*A bluff?*
*I fell off a bluff?!*

A wave of someone else's memories slammed into my head like a sack of river rocks:

*Name: Jin. Fifteen years old.*
*Location: Southfall Village. A miserable, flea-bitten frontier settlement on an isolated coastal island.*
*Parents: Daesung and Yeon.*
*Cause of death: The little idiot was chasing a stray goat near the perimeter fence, slipped on wet moss, and tumbled down a ten-foot ditch.*

*Ten feet?!*

My left eye started twitching violently.

*I am Chung Jin! The Sword Saint of the Northern Heaven! I dueled lightning dragons! I traded blows with demon sovereigns! I commanded the vanguard of Kurukshetra! And I died because I tripped on a pebble chasing a goat?!*

"Damn you, Samsara," I whispered into the moldy straw, my teeth grinding so hard they clicked. "Damn you, King of Hell. When I die again, I'm going to punch you right in the teeth."

"Jin? Are you muttering to ghosts?" Daesung asked, hurrying over and kneeling beside the mat. He blew gently on the wooden bowl, holding it to my lips. "Drink this. It's willow bark and boiled bitter-root. It will stop the swelling in your skull."

I took a sip. It tasted like mud mixed with swamp water. I made a face like I'd swallowed a toad, but my dry throat greedily gulped it down.

"Father," I croaked, testing the strange, reedy voice.

"What is it, son?"

"The plains," I asked, my heart suddenly squeezing tight. "The Great War. The black banners. What happened?!"

Daesung stopped. He exchanged a bewildered, sorrowful look with Yeon.

"Jin-ah," Yeon murmured gently, stroking my sweaty hair. "The fever has confused you. There are no armies here."

"Answer me!" I snapped, a sliver of my old grandmaster bark slipping through before I could swallow it.

Daesung flinched slightly at the authority in my tone, but he just sighed and rubbed his coarse stubble.

"The Great War, Jin? That ended two hundred years ago. The old dynasties tore each other to pieces and collapsed into the sea. There are no grand wars now. Only the wild, the beasts outside the palisade, and the village."

*Two hundred years.*
