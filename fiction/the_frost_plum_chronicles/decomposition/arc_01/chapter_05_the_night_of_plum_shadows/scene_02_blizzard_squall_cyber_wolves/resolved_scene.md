### The Blizzard Squall & Iron-Jaw Wolves

The blizzard outside was a howling wall of white needles. Down the village lane, the wattle fences were burning, their flames whipped flat by the gale into trails of orange sparks. The screams of terror from the outlying huts were instantly swallowed by the roar of the storm.

*CRASH.*

The rear window of the cottage exploded inward in a shower of splintered pine and ice!

A massive, slate-grey predator leaped through the opening. It was an iron-jaw wolf—a bio-engineered war hound whose mandible had been replaced by pneumatic steel shears that snapped shut with the force of an industrial press. Its eyes glowed dull infrared beneath a scarified skull plate.

Daesung shouted, grabbing his woodcutting maul to shield Yeon.

Jin moved.

He didn't grab his oak stick. He snatched two heavy iron kitchen cleavers from the cutting board beside the hearth.

*"Stay behind me!"* Jin roared, his voice stripping away every trace of the sickly boy and detonating with the ancient majesty of the Peerless Sword Saint.

The iron-jaw wolf lunged, its pneumatic jaws gaping to shear Jin's shoulder in two.

*"Dog of the Moon,"* Jin snarled, stepping straight into the beast's attack arc. *"Die!"*
