### Barley Porridge by the Hearth
**Composite Score:** 0.962

---

The hearth in Daesung's cottage roared with dry pine logs, casting a warm, golden glow across the clay walls. Outside, the night was black as pitch, with a furious Himalayan blizzard howling off the bay, rattling the shutters with gusts of freezing snow. On the hearth-hook hung a heavy iron pot of thick barley porridge, bubbling gently and smelling of dried mountain plums, roasted sesame, and wild honey.

Yeon ladled the steaming porridge into wooden bowls, her eyes shining with tears of quiet, disbelieving joy. 

*"Eat, Jin-ah,"* she murmured, placing two sweet plums into his bowl. *"The grain wagons arrived at dusk. The whole village is eating hot porridge tonight. Your father has been laughing like a boy."*

Daesung sat cross-legged by the hearth, holding a bowl with both hands, his chest rising and falling in deep, unbroken peace. *"I don't know where you learned to read ledgers like a scholar, son. But today... for the first time in twenty years, I felt like a man."*

Jin blew on his porridge, his heart aching with an unfamiliar, piercing warmth. He had fought on cosmic flagships; he had stood beside gods; but sitting by this smoky hearth, watching two gentle mortals smile over a bowl of coarse grain, he understood the truth Kamina had shouted from the prow: *Home isn't dirt. It's trust.*

Jin swallowed the porridge. The hot carbohydrate slurry hit his stomach like liquid fuel; his liver greedily absorbed the glucose, funneling energy into his starving bone marrow.

Suddenly, Jin froze.

His spoon stopped an inch from his lips.

Across the howling blizzard outside, his keen ears caught a sound that no mortal hunter could distinguish: the faint, high-frequency whine of automated servos, accompanied by the wet, rhythmic padding of twelve heavy quadrupeds circling the perimeter wall.
