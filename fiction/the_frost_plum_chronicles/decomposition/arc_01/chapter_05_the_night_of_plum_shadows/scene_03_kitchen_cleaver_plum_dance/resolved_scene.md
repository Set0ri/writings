The snowy lane was illuminated by burning thatch and the bloody glare of torches. Black Wave cutthroats, clad in spiked armor, were kicking open granary doors when the shadow of the dried anchovy boy fell across the drifts.

Jin stepped barefoot into the freezing snow.

Compensating for zero internal Qi with surgical bone alignment and rotational torque, he unleashed the **Twenty-Four Movement Plum Blossom Sword Art** through two kitchen cleavers.

The blades blurred in the blizzard like falling pink petals.

*SHING. CHOP. SLICE.*

He deflected an incoming iron spear with the flat of his left cleaver, spun through the blind spot, and drove the right cleaver through the bandit's collarbone. He pivoted on his heel, catching a cyber-wolf mid-leap, shearing its pneumatic jaw cables with a vicious cross-cut that sprayed black hydraulic fluid across the snow drifts.

Within sixty heartbeats, seven bandits and three war hounds lay dead in the slush.

The surviving bandits dropped their torches and fled into the blizzard, screaming that the village was haunted by an ancient demon.

Jin stood in the falling snow, blood dripping from the cleaver hilts, his breath pluming like white smoke in the gale. Through the storm mist at the edge of the woods, he caught the faint red flash of an infrared optical lens retreating toward the coast.

*"Kang,"* Jin whispered, wiping a smear of wolf oil from his cheek. *"Now we settle the debt."*
