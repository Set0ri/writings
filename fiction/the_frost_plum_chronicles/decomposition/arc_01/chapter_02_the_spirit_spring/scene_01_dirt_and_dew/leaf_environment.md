# Leaf Node: Environment (E*)
**Scene:** Eating Dirt & The Gentle Breath of the Morning Dew

The first law of martial arts is simple: a sword cannot cut without an arm behind it. And right now, my arm belonged to a fifteen-year-old who got winded pulling up his trousers. The packed dirt of the floor was freezing, smelling of damp earth, old chimney ash, and dried river reeds. Between my straw mat and the door sat a chipped clay bowl: pale, watery broth where three shriveled minnows floated among strips of boiled marsh-weed, traded at the wharf for our household's precious evening salt.
