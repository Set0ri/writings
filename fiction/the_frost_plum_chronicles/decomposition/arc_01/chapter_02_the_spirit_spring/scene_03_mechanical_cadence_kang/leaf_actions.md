Below him, Uncle Kang walked the palisade catwalk.

Jin's sword-saint senses locked onto Kang's gait. His eyes widened.

Kang took forty-eight paces across the platform. Each pace measured precisely 78.4 centimeters. Each footfall landed at intervals of exactly 1.150 seconds. His left arm swung through an arc of 22 degrees; his head swiveled 45 degrees to the left, paused for 3.0 seconds, and snapped 45 degrees to the right. He had walked the line in the freezing rain for two hours without blinking a single time.
