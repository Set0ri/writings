### The Mechanical Cadence of Uncle Kang
**Composite Score:** 0.962

---

At the northern palisade of Southfall, the crude log ramparts looked out over the salt marshes and the jagged black reefs of the outer bay. Sleet was falling again, coating the sharpened pine stakes in glistening rime. Four village watchmen huddled around a smoky brazier, clutching rusted spears with shivering hands.

Concealed in the upper boughs of a dead cedar forty paces away, Jin crouched motionless, his half-opened dantian siphoning the cold wind to mask his biological heat.

Below him, Uncle Kang walked the palisade catwalk.

Jin's sword-saint senses locked onto Kang's gait. His eyes widened.

Kang took forty-eight paces across the platform. Each pace measured precisely 78.4 centimeters. Each footfall landed at intervals of exactly 1.150 seconds. His left arm swung through an arc of 22 degrees; his head swiveled 45 degrees to the left, paused for 3.0 seconds, and snapped 45 degrees to the right. He had walked the line in the freezing rain for two hours without blinking a single time.

*Solenoids,* Jin thought, a chill far colder than the winter sleet sinking into his marrow. *Servomotors and predictive loops.*

Underneath the greasy rabbit-skin coat and the soft, flabby human flesh, Kang's movements were governed by the ruthless, cold arithmetic of a machine. 

*"Unit 409-Epsilon,"* Jin muttered, remembering the imperial codices from before the catastrophe. *"An AI skin-walker. The Moon isn't just watching Southfall from orbit... they have automated wardens walking the mud, farming human misery like a training model."*

Jin's wolf-grin returned, dark, lethal, and colder than the abyss.

*"You picked the wrong village to run your little simulation, scrap-metal."*
