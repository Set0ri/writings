**Scene:** The Father's Filial Chains
**Score:** 0.961

Daesung was on his knees by the splitting log, coughing into a rag stained with flecks of dark blood. 

*"Father,"* Jin said quietly, stepping through the wattle gate.

Daesung quickly tucked the bloody rag into his belt, forcing a strained, exhausted smile onto his weathered face. *"Jin-ah... you shouldn't be out in the cold. Your fever only broke yesterday."*

*"Why do you take it?"* Jin demanded, leaning on his oak branch. *"You have the broad shoulders of a veteran hunter. Kang is your younger brother, yet he treats you worse than a stray mule. Throw him into the manure ditch and be done with it."*

Daesung's eyes turned dim and hollow. *"You don't understand, son. On her deathbed, your grandmother took my hands. She made me swear by the ancestral tablets: 'Daesung, Kang was born frail. Protect him. Endure him. Never raise a hand against your blood.' A man without filial honor is no better than an animal, Jin."*
