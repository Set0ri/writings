# Chapter 6: The Autopsy of an Infiltrator

The muster yard was an abattoir.

The fire from the burning grain barns cast long, jagged shadows across the mud, turning the center of Southfall into a pit of red hell. Thick, greasy smoke choked the air, smelling of burning barley, scorched flesh, and the sulfuric tang of pitch.

At the eastern palisade breach, forty Black Wave Bandits had swarmed through the shattered sluice gate, waving curved scimitars and shouting bloodthirsty oaths. 

But they weren't in control.

Behind them, leaping through the burning wreckage of the wooden stakes, were three massive, six-legged beasts—**Iron-Jaw Shadow Wolves**. 

Their coarse coats were the color of wet slate, their shoulders standing as high as an ox. Long strings of viscous, black saliva dripped from jaws reinforced with exterior bone plates that could crack an oak shield like a walnut. Driven mad by the scent of roasted grain and fresh human blood, the wolves were tearing through bandit and villager alike.

"Hold the ditch!" Daesung roared, his voice echoing over the screaming.

My father was a terrifying sight. Knee-deep in bloody clay, his linen tunic ripped to shreds, he held a seven-foot boar-spear locked under his arm. 

A shadow-wolf lunged at two teenage village boys huddled against a grain cart. Daesung didn't hesitate. He stepped into the beast's path, planting the iron butt of his spear against a sunken boulder and dropping his weight.

*CRUNCH.*

The charging wolf impaled itself on the heavy iron head. The impact lifted Daesung six inches off the ground, the wood groaning under the force, but my father’s boots bit into the mud and held. With a feral roar of pure, paternal fury, he twisted the spear-shaft, cracking the monster's spine and kicking the dying carcass into the ditch.

"Fall back to the stone well!" Daesung bellowed to the boys, his chest heaving like a forge bellows. "Run!"
