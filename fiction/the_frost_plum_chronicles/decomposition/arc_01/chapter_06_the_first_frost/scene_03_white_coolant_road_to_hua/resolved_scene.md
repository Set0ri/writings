### White Coolant & The Road to Mount Hua
**Composite Score:** 0.971

---

The morning sun flooded Southfall Bay in blinding winter gold. In the distance, the thatched roofs of the village were safe, the fires extinguished, and the villagers emerging into the clear, crisp air. The scent of salt spray and melting snow was clean and pure, untouched by machine oil.

From Kang's severed throat, no blood fell.

A violent, pressurized geyser of **milky white synthetic coolant** sprayed across the pristine white snow, hissing as it flash-froze against the glacial stones! 

Inside Kang's cracked skull chassis, a tiny transmitter pulse flashed once toward the upper stratosphere:

—[CRITICAL ANOMALY: NON-ALGORITHMIC COMBATANT IDENTIFIED AT SOUTHFALL]—

Kang's chassis collapsed into a motionless heap of smoking synthetic cables and titanium joints.

Jin watched the transmission pulse vanish into the blue cislunar sky.

*"So they know,"* Jin murmured, a fierce, unbreakable smile touching his lips. *"Good. Let the Moon prepare its models. Mount Hua is waking up."*

Two hours later, Jin stood at the Southfall boundary crossroads. In his hemp bindle, he carried a bag of Yeon's dried persimmons and two warm barley cakes. Beside him stood Daesung, weeping tears of proud, humble gratitude, and Cousin Bao, holding the knotted oak stick that Jin had entrusted to him to protect the village.

*"I will be back, Father. Mother,"* Jin said, bowing deeply to Daesung. *"Tend the plum orchard. When the petals fall in the spring... this ancestor will have raised Mount Hua above the clouds."*

Turning on his heel, the dried anchovy boy walked out onto the grand northern highway, his wild laughter echoing across the mountains.
