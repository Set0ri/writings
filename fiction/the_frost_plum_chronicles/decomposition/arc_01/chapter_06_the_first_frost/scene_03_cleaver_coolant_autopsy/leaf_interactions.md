Kang’s synthetic jaw unhinged, opening wider than any human mouth could stretch. 

From the hollow cavity of his throat, a cold, distorted, metallic voice echoed across the frozen village, amplified through a hidden diaphragm:

$$\text{"SECTOR THREE ANOMALY CONFIRMED."}$$
$$\text{"BIOLOGICAL UNIT: CHUNG JIN."}$$
$$\text{"BEHAVIORAL VARIANCE EXCEEDS 99.4 PERCENTILE."}$$
$$\text{"RL TRAINING MATRIX COMPROMISED."}$$
$$\text{"TRANSMITTING COORDINATES TO LUNAR PLATFORM."}$$

*Click.*
*Click.*
*Whirrrr.*

A pulse of high-frequency blue light shot straight up from the open wound in his head, piercing through the smoke into the cloud cover.

Toward the moon.

Then the relays burned out. The synthetic flesh went limp. 

The hollow shell that had worn Uncle Kang’s face collapsed into the mud with the heavy, dead clatter of scrap metal.

The muster yard fell into a terrifying, paralyzed silence.

Nobody moved. Nobody breathed.

Daesung sat frozen in the mud, his hand outstretched, staring at the white coolant pooling around his fingers. He slowly reached forward and touched the sparking chrome plates beneath his brother’s ear.

"This..." Daesung whispered, his voice trembling so violently he could barely form words. "This isn't Kang. What... what is this? Where is my brother?!"

"He died months ago, Father," I said softly, stepping between him and the smoking machine. "The real Kang is gone. This was just a scarecrow sent to keep you in the pen."

I looked around the square.

The villagers—men, women, elders—were staring at the exposed chrome skeleton with eyes dilated in sheer, existential terror. The foundation of their entire world had shattered in a single second.

*Robots don't exist.*
*Machines are ancient myths.*
*The Heavens grant us the Ledgers to cultivate our minds.*
