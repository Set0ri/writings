### The Entrance of the Dried Anchovy
**Composite Score:** 0.962

---

The silence that fell over the council hall was absolute, broken only by the rhythmic tap-tap-tap of a knotted wooden stick on the polished flagstones. Snowflakes drifted through the doorway, melting upon the heated stone floor like dying moths.

Down the central aisle strolled Jin.

He wore an oversized hemp tunic that flapped around his paper-thin frame like sails on an abandoned skiff. In his left hand, he held a stalk of dried barley, chewing on it with insolent leisure. In his right hand, he balanced his three-foot knotted oak branch, using it as a cane with exaggerated, dramatic decrepitude.

Beside him walked Cousin Bao, scrubbed clean, his head held low, carrying a water-stained leather satchel under his arm.

"Jin?!" Daesung gasped in horror. "What are you doing out of bed?!"

*"Taking a walk, Father,"* Jin said cheerfully, waving the oak stick at the elders like a schoolteacher greeting rowdy toddlers. *"The air in our shack was getting stuffy, and I heard there was an auction of ancestral graves going on."*

Kang's eyes narrowed, his left hand dropping to the hilt of his short sword. *"Insolent brat. Guards, remove this lunatic before he defiles the tribunal."*

Two burly militia guards stepped forward with iron truncheons raised.

Jin didn't even look at them. *"Touch me, and I'll have the Magistrate's Tax Inspector hang you from the palisade by your own belt straps. Bao—throw the book at him."*
