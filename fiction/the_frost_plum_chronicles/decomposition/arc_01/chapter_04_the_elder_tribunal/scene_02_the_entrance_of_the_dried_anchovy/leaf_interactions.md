"Jin?!" Daesung gasped in horror. "What are you doing out of bed?!"

*"Taking a walk, Father,"* Jin said cheerfully, waving the oak stick at the elders like a schoolteacher greeting rowdy toddlers. *"The air in our shack was getting stuffy, and I heard there was an auction of ancestral graves going on."*

Kang's eyes narrowed, his left hand dropping to the hilt of his short sword. *"Insolent brat. Guards, remove this lunatic before he defiles the tribunal."*

Two burly militia guards stepped forward with iron truncheons raised.

Jin didn't even look at them. *"Touch me, and I'll have the Magistrate's Tax Inspector hang you from the palisade by your own belt straps. Bao—throw the book at him."*
