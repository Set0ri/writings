### The Great Reversal: 300 Sacks of Grain
**Composite Score:** 0.965

---

The hall grew suffocatingly warm as the villagers outside pushed through the doorway, their murmurs growing into an angry, mutinous roar. The smell of burning tallow mingled with the stench of sudden sweat from Uncle Kang's forehead.

Bao slammed the leather satchel onto the council table.

Out spilled a bundle of water-logged ledgers, bound with hemp twine and stamped with the personal signet of the Black Wave Bandits.

Kang's chief enforcer, a scarred brute named Gwan, lunged forward with a drawn iron dagger to seize the papers.

Jin didn't move his feet. With a lightning flick of his wrist, he redirected the oak branch through a clean three-inch arc, tapping the funny-bone on Gwan's elbow.

*CRACK.*

The enforcer shrieked in agony as his ulnar nerve seized; the dagger flew from his numb fingers, burying itself in the council rafters, while Gwan crashed face-first through a decorative cedar screen.

Jin planted his oak branch on top of the ledgers, leaning forward with his terrifying Mount Hua tiger-grin.

*"Let's do some arithmetic, Uncle,"* Jin sang out, his voice ringing across the rafters. *"Fifteen tons of communal barley smuggled to the Gull's Maw sea caves over three seasons. Four crates of weapon-grade iron traded for private gold ingots. It seems the Sector Administration isn't owed thirty-four silver pieces... the Sector Administration owes this village three hundred sacks of emergency winter grain!"*

The elders erupted in uproar. Kang's jaw clicked violently, his eyes flashing with microscopic bursts of static violet light as his internal processors calculated the legal collapse.

*"Sign the release, Uncle,"* Jin whispered, leaning close enough to smell the faint, metallic scent of cold coolant behind Kang's ears. *"Cancel the debt, deed the orchard to my father, and deliver three hundred sacks by sunset tomorrow... or I burn these ledgers right on the Magistrate's desk."*

Kang's fingers trembled, his pen scratching across the parchment with frantic, mechanical desperation.
