### The Council Hall Reckoning
**Composite Score:** 0.952

---

The Southfall Council Hall was the only tiled structure in the settlement, built from heavy fir timbers with stone hearths at either end. Braziers burned with green pine charcoal, casting flickering shadows across the faces of seven village elders seated on low bamboo mats. Outside, the villagers huddled in the freezing sleet, peering through the open doors with anxious, hollow eyes.

Uncle Kang stood at the center of the hall, clad in an immaculate silk-trimmed coat, holding an open parchment roll.

*"Elders,"* Kang proclaimed, his voice resonant and smooth as oiled brass. *"The security levy for the northern palisade is delinquent by thirty-four silver pieces. My brother Daesung has failed to meet his woodcutting quota for three consecutive cycles. Under the founding charter, his cottage, timber plot, and the ancestral plum orchard must be transferred to the Sector Administration to cover the debt."*

Daesung stood before the council, his head bowed, his calloused hands trembling against his coarse linen trousers.

*"Brother Kang,"* Daesung rasped, his voice breaking. *"That orchard was planted by our grandfather. It's the only land our family has left..."*

*"The charter does not recognize sentiment, Daesung,"* Kang said coldly, without a flicker of emotion. *"Only arithmetic."*

The elders looked down at their laps, muttering among themselves in cowardly silence. None dared oppose the man who controlled the militia and the winter grain rations.

Elder Song, his whiskers yellowed with pipe smoke, raised the wooden gavel to ratify the foreclosure.

*BANG.*

The heavy double doors of the hall flew wide open, banging flat against the timber pillars as a blast of icy wind extinguished three braziers.
