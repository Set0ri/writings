Behind the fish-drying sheds, the narrow alley was slick with freezing gray clay and smelled of salted cod, old brine, and rotting wood. Rainwater dripped from the cedar eaves in steady, drumming beads, pooling in ruts carved by ox carts. It was a miserable corner of the world, hidden from the main muster yard—a perfect spot for a bully to throw a beating, or for a reincarnated grandmaster to teach an idiot the laws of gravity.
