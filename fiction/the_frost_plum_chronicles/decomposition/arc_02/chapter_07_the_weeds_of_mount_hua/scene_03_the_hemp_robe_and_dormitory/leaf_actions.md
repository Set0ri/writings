A cosmic war, a dying brother's roar, a solar lotus detonating in the black void... and now, the cold night air of Mount Hua filled his lungs once more. Closing his eyes, he initiated the quiet, steady rhythm of the Morning Dew breath, circulating trace Qi through his calcified meridians.
