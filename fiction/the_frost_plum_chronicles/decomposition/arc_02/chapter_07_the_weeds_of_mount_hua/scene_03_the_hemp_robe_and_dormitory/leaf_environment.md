# Leaf Node: Environment (E*)
**Scene:** The Hemp Robe & The Third-Class Dormitory

Night settled upon Mount Hua, biting and needle-sharp. The third-class dormitory was little more than a drafty wooden lodge where cross-gales whistled through torn paper lattice screens, dropping the room temperature down to near freezing. Ten thin straw pallets lined the bare cedar floor, where nine teenage orphans huddled beneath coarse, lumpy quilts, shivering in their sleep with teeth clicking like dry castanets. The iron woodstove sat hollow and cold, holding only ancient birch ash, while pale blue moonlight spilled across the floorboards in cold, indifferent stripes.
