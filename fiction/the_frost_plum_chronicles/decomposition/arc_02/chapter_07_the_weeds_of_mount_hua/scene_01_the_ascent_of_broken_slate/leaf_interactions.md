# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The Ascent of Broken Slate
**Council Score:** Total 0.947 (Canon: 0.97, Kinetics: 0.92, Sensory: 0.91, Feral: 0.99)

---

At the crumbling gatehouse, two shivering teenage boys in patched, threadbare grey robes fumbled with their chipped iron scabbards, their footwork as unsteady as newborn fawns. Their uniforms were bleached nearly colorless from endless washings, the embroidered plum blossoms faded to ghostly grey smudges.

"H-halt!" the taller boy stammered, raising a pitted practice sword with trembling wrists. "Mount Hua is... we are not accepting guests! Go back down the mountain!"

Jin stopped. He stared at the two starving boys. His left eyebrow twitched. Then, with agonizing slowness, he reached out and poked the taller boy's shin with the tip of his knotted oak stick.

*Thwack.*

"Ow! What was that for?!"

"Look at your grip!" Jin roared, his reedy voice echoing off the canyon walls with the fury of a thousand frustrated ancestors. "Are you holding a sword or are you trying to tickle a dying trout?! Your center of gravity is higher than a mountain pheasant! If an enemy blew on your foreheads you'd tumble all the way down to Shaanxi! Where is your Sect Leader? Where are the elders?! Go tell them their ancestor woke up and he's going to break every broom on this mountain over their heads!"
