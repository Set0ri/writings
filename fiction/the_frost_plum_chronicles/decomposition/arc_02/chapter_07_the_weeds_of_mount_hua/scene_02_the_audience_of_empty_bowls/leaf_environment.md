**Scene:** The Audience of Empty Bowls
**Council Score:** Total 0.931 (Canon: 0.94, Kinetics: 0.89, Sensory: 0.93, Feral: 0.97)

The Grand Hall of Mount Hua was fifty paces wide, built of mountain cedar that had once withstood the celestial wars—now reduced to a cavern of shadows and shame. Cold autumn rainwater leaked through fractured terracotta roof tiles, dripping with maddening, funerary cadence into chipped earthenware bowls lined along the flagstones: *plink... plonk... plink.* The air was heavy with the smell of damp mold, bitter chicory roots, and old, suffocating defeat. On the high northern wall, the magnificent silk banner of the Plum Blossom Sect was faded to grey, its golden embroidery long since picked clean and sold for rice. A single iron dish lamp sputtered with cheap, foul-smelling rendered tallow, barely illuminating the two old men huddled by a brazier of ash.
