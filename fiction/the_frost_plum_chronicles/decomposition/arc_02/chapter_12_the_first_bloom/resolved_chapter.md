### The Wooden Stick Blooms

And then, Mount Hua bloomed.

It was not a solitary petal. It was a tempest. A blinding, roaring blizzard of ten thousand radiant pink plum blossoms detonated into existence across the central arena, forged of pure non-computable Qi, indomitable biological will, and five thousand years of unbroken memory. The fragrance was overwhelming—the wild, fierce, ozone-laced scent of ancient plum blossoms exploding through winter ice, obliterating the bitter stench of grease, mud, and greed. The entire mountain seemed to roar with celestial thunder, drowning out the shrieks of the crowd beneath a hurricane of falling petals.

Song Won, his vanity inflamed to murderous fury, lunged with the full speed of his elite Southern Edge cultivation—the "Absolute Piercing Wave," aiming directly for Jin's throat.

Jin did not retreat by a single inch. 

His lips curled into that wide, savage, unhinged wolf-grin that had terrified warlords five millennia ago. He stepped forward into the path of the sword, his knotted winter oak branch moving not in a line, but in a breathtaking, non-computable cascade.

*CLANG-KABOOM!* 

The humble dead branch met the Damascus steel blade. The non-linear Qi frequency traveled down the steel like an earthquake. In a blinding flash of pink light, Song Won's treasured family sword shattered into forty jagged steel fragments that rained harmlessly across the pavers. 

Before the Southern Edge prodigy could even register the empty hilt in his hand, Jin’s stick blurred through the petal storm.

*THWACK-CRACK!*

The knot of the branch struck Song Won across the forehead, lifting the hundred-and-sixty-pound swordsman clean off his feet. Song Won spun like a top through the air, crashing directly into Elder Jinbaek's chest with the force of a battering ram, sending both master and disciple tumbling head-over-heels off the four-foot arena stage into a muddy ditch below!

The entire arena was struck dumb with sacred, paralyzing awe.

Jin stood alone at the center of the ring, the notched winter oak branch resting across his shoulders, his ragged hemp robe fluttering in the scented breeze. Pink Qi petals drifted down through the autumn sunlight like holy snow, blanketing the stage, the shattered steel, and the cheering spectators.

Below the stage, in the mud ditch, Elder Jinbaek and Song Won groaned in humiliated defeat. The two hundred watching martial artists and merchant bosses erupted into thunderous, world-shaking roars of acclaim, while merchant guild masters frantically shoved their debt ledgers into their pockets, desperate to disassociate from Southern Edge.

Behind Jin, Sect Leader Hyun Jong collapsed to his knees on the veranda, weeping tears of religious ecstasy as Elder Hwang clutched his heart in pure joy. Baek Cheon, Yu Iseol, Jo Gul, and Yoon Jong bounded onto the platform, their eyes blazing with pride, their steel swords held high toward the heavens.

"MOUNT HUA!" the disciples roared in unison, their voices echoing across the Shaanxi peaks like a returning thunderclap.

Jin looked up toward the vast blue sky. For a fleeting instant, through the drifting pink petals, he saw the faint, grinning image of his fallen older brother Kamina, giving him a fiery thumbs-up from beyond the stars.

Jin’s wolf-grin softened into a rare, peaceful smile.

*"We're just getting started, Brother,"* Jin whispered. *"Mount Hua is back."*
