High noon struck Lotus Blossom Peak with the blinding, unforgiving glare of autumn sun. In the vast, weed-choked central sparring arena, the white-and-silver banners of the Southern Edge Sect snapped in the canyon wind like predatory raptors circling a dying beast. Thirty young swordsmen stood in immaculate military formation, their embroidered purple silk vests, gilded breastplates, and mirror-polished Damascus steel scabbards throwing blinding sun glares into the eyes of the watching crowd. Over two hundred regional elders, merchant syndicate bosses, and martial sect delegates packed the perimeter galleries, their low murmurs thick with vultures' anticipation: today was the day Southern Edge had publicly summoned Mount Hua to forfeit its ancestral domain.
