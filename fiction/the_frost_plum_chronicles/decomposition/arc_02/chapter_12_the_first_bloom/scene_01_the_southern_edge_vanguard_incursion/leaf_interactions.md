"Do you look upon us and see only beggars?" Sect Leader Hyun Jong spoke from the steps, his gentle voice trembling with generations of suppressed sorrow.

Elder Jinbaek chuckled—a harsh, barking sneer that echoed off the quartz walls. "Beggars? No, Sect Leader Hyun. We see walking ghosts who have outlived their welcome. The Central Plains has no need for a sect of traitors and paupers. Surrender Lotus Peak to Southern Edge, and we will permit your surviving disciples to sweep our stable yards for three meals a day!"

*FLIP. PLOP.*

A piece of roasted sweet potato skin sailed through the air, landing with a wet, sticky smack directly atop Elder Jinbaek's embroidered silk boot.

"Hey. Old goat with the goat beard," a voice called out, lazy and reedy as a summer mosquito. "You talk awfully loud for a thief wearing stolen pants."
