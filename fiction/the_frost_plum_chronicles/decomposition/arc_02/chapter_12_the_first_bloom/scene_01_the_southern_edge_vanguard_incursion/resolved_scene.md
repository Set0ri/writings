### The Southern Edge Vanguard Incursion
**Composite Council Score:** 0.949

---

High noon struck Lotus Blossom Peak with the blinding, unforgiving glare of autumn sun. In the vast, weed-choked central sparring arena, the white-and-silver banners of the Southern Edge Sect snapped in the canyon wind like predatory raptors circling a dying beast. Thirty young swordsmen stood in immaculate military formation, their embroidered purple silk vests, gilded breastplates, and mirror-polished Damascus steel scabbards throwing blinding sun glares into the eyes of the watching crowd. Over two hundred regional elders, merchant syndicate bosses, and martial sect delegates packed the perimeter galleries, their low murmurs thick with vultures' anticipation: today was the day Southern Edge had publicly summoned Mount Hua to forfeit its ancestral domain.

Southern Edge Elder Jinbaek raised a bejeweled hand. In razor-sharp unison, thirty disciples drew their weapons in a single blinding fraction of a second—the synchronized shriek of folded steel ringing across the mountain like a war horn. At the vanguard, eighteen-year-old sword prodigy Song Won stepped into the center of the chalked dueling ring. With an ostentatious flourish clearly rehearsed before a palace mirror, he spun his damascened blade through a triple flower twist, planting the tip into the stone with a haughty sneer: "Mount Hua! Send forth your disciples to receive instruction! Or sign the deed of forfeiture and crawl down the mountain on all fours!"

On the shaded veranda, Jin sat cross-legged atop a railing, noisily chewing on a cold roasted sweet potato, his eyes half-closed in utter, bored indifference.

"Do you look upon us and see only beggars?" Sect Leader Hyun Jong spoke from the steps, his gentle voice trembling with generations of suppressed sorrow.

Elder Jinbaek chuckled—a harsh, barking sneer that echoed off the quartz walls. "Beggars? No, Sect Leader Hyun. We see walking ghosts who have outlived their welcome. The Central Plains has no need for a sect of traitors and paupers. Surrender Lotus Peak to Southern Edge, and we will permit your surviving disciples to sweep our stable yards for three meals a day!"

*FLIP. PLOP.*

A piece of roasted sweet potato skin sailed through the air, landing with a wet, sticky smack directly atop Elder Jinbaek's embroidered silk boot.

"Hey. Old goat with the goat beard," a voice called out, lazy and reedy as a summer mosquito. "You talk awfully loud for a thief wearing stolen pants."
