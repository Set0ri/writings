### Senior Brother Han's Psychological Dismantling
**Composite Council Score:** 0.953

---

A dead, suffocating hush swept through the two hundred spectators packed into the galleries. The whispered chatter of merchant guild masters died in their throats like snuffed candles. Out from Mount Hua's veranda strode Senior Brother Han. He wore a pristine robe of deep midnight indigo, his long black sleeves billowing in the thermal drafts with the effortless majesty of a soaring dragon. The sunlight seemed to pool around him, giving his statuesque, unblinking face the terrifying, carved perfection of a celestial executioner.

Han stopped three paces before Elder Jinbaek. He folded his hands into his voluminous sleeves, tilted his head slightly, and narrowed his eyes into that fabled, terrifying "Dragon's Glare." Because he was severely near-sighted and couldn't actually see Jinbaek's face without squinting, the elder interpreted the cold, unblinking gaze as the appraisal of a god deciding which limb to amputate first.

*(Merciful heavens, Queen of the Moon, please let Jin's gossip be true!)* Han wept silently in the churning, panicked void of his soul. *(If this elder lunges at me, I cannot even dodge! I will be sliced into ribbons before I can scream!)*

Outwardly, Han let out a long, heavy, aristocratic sigh that dripped with infinite pity.

"Elder Jinbaek," Han intoned, his deep baritone carrying effortlessly to every corner of the arena. "You speak of swordsmanship, yet your left meridian pathway has been fifty percent occluded ever since your shameful failure with the stolen 'Seventeen Waves' scripture three months ago. Your spleen Qi is stagnant, your liver pulse is erratic, and your internal energy leaks like a cracked chamber pot. You brought thirty disciples to Mount Hua not to conquer us... but because you are terrified that if you fight alone, your dantian will rupture in front of the entire province."

Elder Jinbaek turned the color of curdled milk. His knees visibly buckled. "You... how... who told you...?!"

The two hundred watching martial artists gasped, leaning forward in stunned disbelief. To the world, Mount Hua's senior disciple had just diagnosed an elder's deepest martial secret with a single glance!

Elder Jinbaek stumbled backward, his left heel catching on the arena stone as beads of cold sweat poured down his neck. He looked around wildly, but the regional martial artists and merchant bosses were already murmuring in disgust, pointing at his trembling hands. 

Han did not even deign to watch the man's humiliation. With agonizing, leisurely poise, he brushed an invisible speck of mountain dust from his lapel, turned his back entirely on Southern Edge, and gestured toward the dueling ring with a casual flick of his long sleeve.

"A mere junior of Mount Hua will dispose of this rubbish," Han announced calmly to the heavens, his majestic voice echoing with sovereign boredom. "I have no desire to soil ancestral ground with the blood of diseased dogs."

Inside his long indigo sleeves, Han's hands were clamped together in white-knuckled terror, his kneecaps vibrating like dry castanets as he walked with agonizing slowness toward the shaded bench, praying to every known deity that he wouldn't trip over his own hem and reveal his drenched socks.
