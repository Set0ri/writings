Guild Master Kang took an involuntary step backward, his embroidered satin boot splashing into icy red mud. The twenty armored enforcers froze, their grip on their maces slick with nervous sweat; not a single man dared cross the courtyard line. Han slowly, leisurely lifted his right sleeve, brushing an invisible speck of mountain dust from his lapel with agonizing poise. To the watching thugs, the gesture was the chilling nonchalance of a demigod preparing to wipe out an ant hill.
