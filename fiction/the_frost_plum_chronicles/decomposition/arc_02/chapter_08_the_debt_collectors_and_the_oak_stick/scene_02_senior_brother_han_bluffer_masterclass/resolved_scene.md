### Senior Brother Han's Bluffer Masterclass
**Composite Council Score:** 0.947

---

The mountain wind ceased with eerie, unnatural suddenness. The heavy sleet seemed to slow in midair, pattering against the flagstones in deadened, muted thuds. A suffocating stillness fell across the courtyard—the kind of breathless dread that precedes an artillery shell or a lightning strike. The two heavy draft horses hitched to Kang's wagons took three panicked steps backward, their breath steaming white as their hooves clattered against the wet stones. Out from the grand pavilion stepped Senior Brother Han.

"Are you finished barking?" Han said.

His voice was a low, velvet baritone that resonated through the damp courtyard like a temple bronze struck at midnight. He stood at the edge of the pavilion, his sleeves folded neatly into his crossed arms, looking down at Guild Master Kang with an expression of such cold, unfathomable pity that the fat merchant reflexively swallowed his tongue.

*(Oh gods, oh Buddha, oh merciful ancestors, where is Jin?!)* Han screamed internally, his heart thrashing against his ribs like a trapped sparrow. *(If that lunatic child doesn't swing his stick in the next three seconds, these thugs are going to turn me into dumpling stuffing! Jin, I will give you my entire silver allowance, just hit someone!)*

Outwardly, Han narrowed his eyes—his severe near-sightedness transforming his squint into the terrifying, soul-piercing "Dragon's Glare" of an ancient grandmaster calculating forty ways to sever their meridians. "Guild Master Kang. You brought twenty men with iron maces. Do you truly believe that is enough to leave this peak alive?"

Guild Master Kang took an involuntary step backward, his embroidered satin boot splashing into icy red mud. The twenty armored enforcers froze, their grip on their maces slick with nervous sweat; not a single man dared cross the courtyard line. Han slowly, leisurely lifted his right sleeve, brushing an invisible speck of mountain dust from his lapel with agonizing poise. Inside the deep silk folds, his knuckles were clamped together so hard they were white, holding his violently trembling fingers still so his sleeves wouldn't vibrate like locust wings. To the watching thugs, the gesture was the chilling nonchalance of a demigod preparing to wipe out an ant hill.
