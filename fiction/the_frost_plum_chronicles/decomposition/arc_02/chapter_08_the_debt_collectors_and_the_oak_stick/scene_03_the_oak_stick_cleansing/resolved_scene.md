### The Oak Stick Cleansing & Reverse Extortion
**Composite Council Score:** 0.959

---

The courtyard erupted into chaos so instantaneous that the sleet seemed to shatter in midair. Mud exploded in violent, four-foot arcs as twenty armored bodies were pulverized into the slurry. The acoustic crack of hardwood on iron rang out like thunder in a bell tower—*THWACK-CRACK-THUMP!* Splintered oak and dented pig-iron tumbled through the mist, while the high, undignified wails of grown men echoed off the crags of Lotus Peak.

Jin moved like a rabid mountain phantom. Darting out from behind the veranda pillar, his knotted winter oak branch struck with terrifying, non-computable precision. 

*CRACK!*

"Trespassing!" Jin roared, the branch snapping the lead thug's mace shaft in half and driving the splintered butt into the man's solar plexus.

*THWACK!*

"Spitting on our steps!" Jin spun on one heel, his emaciated body bending like willow, driving the knot of the branch into the second enforcer's kneecap with a sickening pop.

*KAPOW!*

"Disturbing your grandfather's nap!" 

In less than fifteen seconds, eighteen grown, armored mercenaries were groaning in the mud, clutching dislocated elbows, broken shins, and fractured jaws. Jin cleared the last six paces in a single leap, driving his muddy straw sandal straight into Guild Master Kang's greasy belly, pinning the fat merchant to the wet flagstones like a pinned beetle.

"Now then, fat little piggy," Jin purred, resting the muddy tip of his oak stick directly against Guild Master Kang's quivering triple chin. "Let's review our accounts."

Kang was weeping openly, greasy tears and snot streaking through the red mud on his cheeks. He looked desperately up at Senior Brother Han on the veranda. "Senior Disciple Han! Noble Han! Mercy! Call off your rabid dog! We are respectable merchants!"

Han stroked his imaginary beard, his expression so aloof and godlike that even the rain seemed to honor him. "Mount Hua is a place of harmony," Han intoned solemnly, fighting down a violent urge to vomit from pure adrenaline. "However, our junior enforces spiritual balance with... commendable thoroughness."

Jin's feral grin widened until his molars gleamed. "Did you hear that? Spiritual balance! You owe Mount Hua five thousand silver taels for illegal wheel ruts, mud cleanup, and spiritual distress. Unload the chests from the wagons. Right now. If I find a single counterfeit ingot, I'll shave your head with a broken roof tile!"

On the veranda, Elder Hwang clutched his heart, his eyes rolling back in ecstatic, religious shock as three heavy iron chests of silver were hauled onto Mount Hua's porch.
