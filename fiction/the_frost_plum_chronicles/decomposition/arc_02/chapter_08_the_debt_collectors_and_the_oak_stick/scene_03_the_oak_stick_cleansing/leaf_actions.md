Jin moved like a rabid mountain phantom. Darting out from behind the veranda pillar, his knotted winter oak branch struck with terrifying, non-computable precision. 

*CRACK!*

"Trespassing!" Jin roared, the branch snapping the lead thug's mace shaft in half and driving the splintered butt into the man's solar plexus.

*THWACK!*

"Spitting on our steps!" Jin spun on one heel, his emaciated body bending like willow, driving the knot of the branch into the second enforcer's kneecap with a sickening pop.

*KAPOW!*

"Disturbing your grandfather's nap!" 

In less than fifteen seconds, eighteen grown, armored mercenaries were groaning in the mud, clutching dislocated elbows, broken shins, and fractured jaws. Jin cleared the last six paces in a single leap, driving his muddy straw sandal straight into Guild Master Kang's greasy belly, pinning the fat merchant to the wet flagstones like a pinned beetle.
