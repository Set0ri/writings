"Now then, fat little piggy," Jin purred, resting the muddy tip of his oak stick directly against Guild Master Kang's quivering triple chin. "Let's review our accounts."

Kang was weeping openly, greasy tears and snot streaking through the red mud on his cheeks. He looked desperately up at Senior Brother Han on the veranda. "Senior Disciple Han! Noble Han! Mercy! Call off your rabid dog! We are respectable merchants!"

Han stroked his imaginary beard, his expression so aloof and godlike that even the rain seemed to honor him. "Mount Hua is a place of harmony," Han intoned solemnly, fighting down a violent urge to vomit from pure adrenaline. "However, our junior enforces spiritual balance with... commendable thoroughness."

Jin's feral grin widened until his molars gleamed. "Did you hear that? Spiritual balance! You owe Mount Hua five thousand silver taels for illegal wheel ruts, mud cleanup, and spiritual distress. Unload the chests from the wagons. Right now. If I find a single counterfeit ingot, I'll shave your head with a broken roof tile!"

On the veranda, Elder Hwang clutched his heart, his eyes rolling back in ecstatic, religious shock as three heavy iron chests of silver were hauled onto Mount Hua's porch.
