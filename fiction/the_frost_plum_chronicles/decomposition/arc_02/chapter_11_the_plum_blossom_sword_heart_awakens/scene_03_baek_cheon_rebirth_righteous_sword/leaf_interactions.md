# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** Baek Cheon's Rebirth as the Righteous Sword

The courtyard was dead still, save for the patter of falling pine splinters drifting down through the golden dawn.

Baek Cheon stood frozen, his chest heaving like a bellows, his sword still humming in his blistered hand. He looked from the pulverized post to his trembling hands, and then slowly turned toward Jin. Without hesitation, the proudest aristocrat of Shaanxi sank to one knee in the mud, bowing his head until his forehead nearly brushed the gravel.

"My sword was blind," Baek Cheon spoke, his voice trembling with tears of profound, ego-shattering catharsis. "I chased empty elegance while our ancestors starved in the dark. Chung Jin... my life, my blade, and my breath belong to the restoration of Mount Hua. Command me as you will."

Jin stood before him, the knotted winter oak branch resting across his small shoulder. He reached down, caught Baek Cheon by the shoulder of his torn tunic, and hauled the older disciple back to his feet with surprising, wiry strength.

"Stand up, Dong-ryong," Jin said, his voice quiet, steady, and blazing with unshakeable conviction. "A swordsman of Mount Hua kneels only to the ancestors. You are the Righteous Sword of this mountain. Hold your head up. Because this afternoon... we take back everything they stole from us."
