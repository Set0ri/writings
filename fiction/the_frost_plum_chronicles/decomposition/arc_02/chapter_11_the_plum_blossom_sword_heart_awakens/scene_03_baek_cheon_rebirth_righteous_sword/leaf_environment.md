**Scene:** Baek Cheon's Rebirth as the Righteous Sword

The first light of dawn broke over the Shaanxi mountains in a surging tide of molten gold and blazing apricot. The sea of autumn mist below Lotus Peak parted like an ocean before a divine decree, revealing the sheer quartz cliffs towering into the golden sky. Baek Cheon’s embroidered silk robes were shredded to ribbons, stripped to the waist and tied around his hips; his porcelain skin was stained with black soot, red granite dust, and dried blood from splintered calluses. Steam rose from his broad, muscular shoulders like white smoke rising from a blacksmith's forge.
