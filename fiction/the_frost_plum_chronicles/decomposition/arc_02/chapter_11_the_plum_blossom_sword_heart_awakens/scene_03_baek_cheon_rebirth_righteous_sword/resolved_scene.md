### Baek Cheon's Rebirth as the Righteous Sword
**Composite Council Score:** 0.956

---

The first light of dawn broke over the Shaanxi mountains in a surging tide of molten gold and blazing apricot. The sea of autumn mist below Lotus Peak parted like an ocean before a divine decree, revealing the sheer quartz cliffs towering into the golden sky. Baek Cheon’s embroidered silk robes were shredded to ribbons, stripped to the waist and tied around his hips; his porcelain skin was stained with black soot, red granite dust, and dried blood from splintered calluses. Steam rose from his broad, muscular shoulders like white smoke rising from a blacksmith's forge.

Baek Cheon planted his bare heels into the shattered flagstones with bone-deep, unshakeable gravity. He did not dance. He did not posture. He drew upon the agonizing burn of fifty-pound boulders, the shame of generational ruin, and the wild, terrifying belief planted in his chest by a demonic junior.

*SHRRRRR-BOOM!* 

His steel sword unleashed a guttural, lion-like roar as it tore through the morning air. The blade oscillated with ferocious, bone-shattering torque—and from the vibrating steel, three colossal, radiant crimson plum blossoms detonated into existence! The fragrant Qi storm surged twenty paces across the courtyard, slamming into an ancient, iron-banded pine training post. 

*KABOOM!*

The foot-thick timber post exploded from within, pulverizing into a blizzard of fragrant white splinters and pink sparks that rained across the entire terrace.

The courtyard was dead still, save for the patter of falling pine splinters drifting down through the golden dawn.

Baek Cheon stood frozen, his chest heaving like a bellows, his sword still humming in his blistered hand. He looked from the pulverized post to his trembling hands, and then slowly turned toward Jin. Without hesitation, the proudest aristocrat of Shaanxi sank to one knee in the mud, bowing his head until his forehead nearly brushed the gravel.

"My sword was blind," Baek Cheon spoke, his voice trembling with tears of profound, ego-shattering catharsis. "I chased empty elegance while our ancestors starved in the dark. Chung Jin... my life, my blade, and my breath belong to the restoration of Mount Hua. Command me as you will."

Jin stood before him, the knotted winter oak branch resting across his small shoulder. He reached down, caught Baek Cheon by the shoulder of his torn tunic, and hauled the older disciple back to his feet with surprising, wiry strength.

"Stand up, Dong-ryong," Jin said, his voice quiet, steady, and blazing with unshakeable conviction. "A swordsman of Mount Hua kneels only to the ancestors. You are the Righteous Sword of this mountain. Hold your head up. Because this afternoon... we take back everything they stole from us."
