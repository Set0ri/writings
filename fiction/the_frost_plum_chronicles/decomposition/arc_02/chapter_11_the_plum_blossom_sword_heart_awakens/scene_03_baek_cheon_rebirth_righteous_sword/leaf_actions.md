# Leaf Node: Actions (A*)
**Scene:** Baek Cheon's Rebirth as the Righteous Sword
**Council Score:** Total 0.959 (Canon: 0.97, Kinetics: 0.96, Sensory: 0.92, Feral: 0.99)

---

Baek Cheon planted his bare heels into the shattered flagstones with bone-deep, unshakeable gravity. He did not dance. He did not posture. He drew upon the agonizing burn of fifty-pound boulders, the shame of generational ruin, and the wild, terrifying belief planted in his chest by a demonic junior.

*SHRRRRR-BOOM!* 

His steel sword unleashed a guttural, lion-like roar as it tore through the morning air. The blade oscillated with ferocious, bone-shattering torque—and from the vibrating steel, three colossal, radiant crimson plum blossoms detonated into existence! The fragrant Qi storm surged twenty paces across the courtyard, slamming into an ancient, iron-banded pine training post. 

*KABOOM!*

The foot-thick timber post exploded from within, pulverizing into a blizzard of fragrant white splinters and pink sparks that rained across the entire terrace.
