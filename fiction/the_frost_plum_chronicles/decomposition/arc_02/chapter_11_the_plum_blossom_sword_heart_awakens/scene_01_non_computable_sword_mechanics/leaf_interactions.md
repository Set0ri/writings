Baek Cheon stared at the spiral in the dirt, his mind reeling as months of rigid aristocratic conditioning dissolved in a single revelation. "If the tip oscillates without fixed trajectory... then the recoil torque cannot be dissipated into the ground. It has to be absorbed entirely by the dantian and radial tendons."

Jin gave an unholy cackle, smacking Baek Cheon's shoulder with the oak stick. "Look at that! The pretty boy actually has a brain between his ears! Why do you think I made you carry fifty-pound boulders up Lotus Peak until you threw up your dinner?! Did you think I was just torturing you for fun?!"

"...Weren't you?" Jo Gul muttered from the snow, rubbing his bruised buttocks.

"Well, ninety percent for fun, ten percent for training!" Jin shot back without shame. "Your forearms are finally dense enough to absorb the shock without snapping your wrist bones! Now stop blubbering!"

Beside them, Yu Iseol drew her plain steel sword. Her unblinking dark eyes reflected the red coals of the brazier. Without a word, she lowered her center of gravity, took a half-step forward, and let her wrist flutter. The blade carved a quiet, trembling curve through the frost, releasing a sound like silk tearing in the dark. Jin watched her, his feral grin softening into quiet awe. "...Look at that. The girl is a monster."
