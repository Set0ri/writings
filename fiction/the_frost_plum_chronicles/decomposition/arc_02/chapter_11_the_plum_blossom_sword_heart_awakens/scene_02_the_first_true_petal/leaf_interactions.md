# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The First True Petal
**Council Score:** Total 0.954 (Canon: 0.97, Kinetics: 0.93, Sensory: 0.92, Feral: 1)

---

Silence reigned on the terrace—a silence so profound that the tears trickling down Yoon Jong's jaw sounded like raindrops on stone.

"The plum blossoms..." Yoon Jong choked, collapsing to his knees, his trembling fingers reaching out to touch the five severed leaf fragments. "They were not poetry... they were not legends... the Plum Blossom Sword truly exists..."

Yu Iseol lowered her sword with slow, reverent care. Her porcelain cheeks were flushed, her unblinking dark eyes fixed on the empty air where the pink petal had vanished. "...It bloomed," she whispered, her voice cracking for the first time since Jin had met her.

Jin stepped forward. He did not yell. He did not swing his stick. He reached out and gently ruffled her untamed, wind-tousled black hair with a rough, calloused hand. 

"Of course it bloomed, you crazy brat," he said, his voice husky with five thousand years of buried pride. "You're a disciple of Mount Hua. It has always lived in your blood."

Then he spun around, his wolf-grin returning like a flash of summer lightning as he leveled his branch at Baek Cheon. "Now! Dong-ryong! Your junior sister just blossomed a flower! If you don't bloom one before sunrise, I will shave off your magnificent aristocratic eyebrows and paint plum blossoms on your forehead! Draw your sword!"
