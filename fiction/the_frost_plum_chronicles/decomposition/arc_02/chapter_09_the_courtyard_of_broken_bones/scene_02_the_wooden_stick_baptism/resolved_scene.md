### The Wooden Stick Baptism
**Composite Council Score:** 0.959

---

The morning mist was instantly shredded into violent ribbons as the courtyard exploded into acoustic violence. The sharp ringing of steel meeting seasoned winter oak echoed off the granite walls like hammer blows in an imperial armory—*CLANG-THWACK-CRACK!* Disciples went tumbling through the air like dry peas tossed into a hot iron pan, splashing face-first into icy mud and scattering shattered practice weapons across the cracked pavers. The white mountain frost was pulverized into mist beneath the manic, dancing shuffle of Jin's frayed straw sandals.

Baek Cheon launched forward with a furious, blinding thrust—the textbook perfection of Mount Hua's Falling Petal Stance. 

Jin didn't even draw a full breath. Shifting his torso three inches to the left, he let the gleaming tip graze the empty air over his shoulder. The knotted winter oak branch whipped down with lightning bio-mechanical torque.

*CRACK!*

"Your wrist is loose!"

The stick pulverized Baek Cheon's radial bone, knocking the steel blade spinning ten feet into the mist. Before Baek Cheon could comprehend the loss, Jin pivoted on his left ball, his branch tracing a tight circle that cracked Baek Cheon across the top of his skull with the sound of a ripe melon splitting open.

*THWACK!*

"Your head is too high!"

A goose egg the size of a pigeon's egg erupted instantly on Baek Cheon's forehead as he went reeling into the dirt. Second-class disciples Jo Gul and Yoon Jong rushed forward with raised weapons—only for Jin to slide beneath their guard like a greased otter. He hooked Jo Gul's ankle with the curved butt of the stick, sending the muscular brawler flying head-first into an empty rainwater barrel with a hollow *BONG!*, then tapped Yoon Jong on the bridge of his nose so sharply the dormitory leader sneezed three times and sat down hard in the gravel.

"Is this all?!"

Jin stood in the center of the courtyard, the knotted oak branch resting casually across his scrawny collarbone, glaring down at twelve groaning disciples clutching their heads, ribs, and bruised shins.

"Twelve grown men! Twelve sword geniuses of the great Mount Hua! Beaten into porridge in thirty seconds by a fifteen-year-old dried anchovy using a dead tree branch! If the ancestors saw this pathetic display, they wouldn't just weep—they'd claw their way out of the dirt and beat you unconscious with their own femurs! Get up! GET UP, YOU SLUGS!"

Baek Cheon struggled to his knees, his noble aristocratic vanity shattered into dust, tears of furious humiliation mixing with the dirt on his cheeks. "You... what kind of demonic art is that...?"

"Demonic art?" Jin snarled, stepping on Baek Cheon's sword blade and snapping the steel clean in half with his heel. "This is Mount Hua's basic balance, you blind idiot! While you were perfuming your robes, Southern Edge was stealing your rice! From today on, anyone who wants to hold a steel sword on this mountain will have to earn it in blood!"

From the edge of the pavilion, second-class disciple Yu Iseol stepped silently into the light. Her pale face showed no anger—only an unblinking, obsessive hunger. She stared at Jin's wooden stick, then bowed low. "...Teach me." 

Jin's eye twitched as he stared at the silent girl. "...Crazy brat. Fine. You get the biggest rock."
