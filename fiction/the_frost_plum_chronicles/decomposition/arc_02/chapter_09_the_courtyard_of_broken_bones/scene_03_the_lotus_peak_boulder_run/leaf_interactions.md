# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The Lotus Peak Boulder Run

"Why... why are we doing this?" Baek Cheon wheezed through cracked, bleeding lips as he reached the two-thousandth stair, his vision tunneling into black dots. "What does carrying rocks have to do with the Plum Blossom Sword...?"

Jin, who had been hopping lightly alongside them carrying his winter branch, slowed his pace. For a fleeting second, the unhinged gremlin vanished, replaced by an ancient swordsman whose gaze carried the weight of five thousand years of fallen comrades.

"The Plum Blossom Sword blooms in the dead of winter," Jin said, his voice quiet, slicing through the freezing gale with sudden, absolute clarity. "It doesn't bloom in summer warmth. It blooms when the frost is thickest and every other branch has withered into dust. Southern Edge stole your manuals, took your silver, and convinced you that you were beggars. But they couldn't steal the stone beneath your feet. Today, you carry this mountain. Tomorrow, this mountain carries you."

Baek Cheon stared at the emaciated boy in stunned, tearful silence. His knees locked. With a ragged, guttural roar that echoed across the Shaanxi canyon, he forced his trembling legs forward, hauling the granite boulder into the fading light.
