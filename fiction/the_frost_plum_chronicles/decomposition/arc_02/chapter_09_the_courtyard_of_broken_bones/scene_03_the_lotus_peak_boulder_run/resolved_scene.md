### The Lotus Peak Boulder Run
**Composite Council Score:** 0.956

---

Sunset ignited the jagged crags of Mount Hua into fangs of burning copper and blood-crimson. Below the precipice, the autumn plains of Shaanxi stretched into freezing violet shadows, while the gale howling up the canyon whipped sweat from the disciples' brows and froze it into brittle rime against their temples. The three thousand granite stairs climbed into the burning clouds like an executioner's road, stained with drops of sweat, torn shoe leather, and the sour bile of exhausted boys pushed past every human limit.

Baek Cheon hauled a fifty-pound granite boulder lashed to his bruised spine with rough hemp cord, each step extracting an agonizing shudder from his trembling quads. The coarse rope bit through his silk tunic, rupturing capillaries and smearing red streaks across the white rock. Behind him, Jo Gul collapsed face-first onto stair fifteen hundred, clutching his shins and sobbing dry tears: "I can't... my lungs are bleeding... just leave me to die..."

*THWACK.*

Jin's oak stick struck Jo Gul's backside with explosive ferocity. "Die? Who gave you permission to die on Mount Hua's stairs?! If you die without my permission, I'll go to the Yellow Springs and drag you back by your ear! Get up, you bowl of cold noodles! Look at Yu Iseol! She's carrying a sixty-pound block and she hasn't even blinked! Crawl!"

"Why... why are we doing this?" Baek Cheon wheezed through cracked, bleeding lips as he reached the two-thousandth stair, his vision tunneling into black dots. "What does carrying rocks have to do with the Plum Blossom Sword...?"

Jin, who had been hopping lightly alongside them carrying his winter branch, slowed his pace. For a fleeting second, the unhinged gremlin vanished, replaced by an ancient swordsman whose gaze carried the weight of five thousand years of fallen comrades.

"The Plum Blossom Sword blooms in the dead of winter," Jin said, his voice quiet, slicing through the freezing gale with sudden, absolute clarity. "It doesn't bloom in summer warmth. It blooms when the frost is thickest and every other branch has withered into dust. Southern Edge stole your manuals, took your silver, and convinced you that you were beggars. But they couldn't steal the stone beneath your feet. Today, you carry this mountain. Tomorrow, this mountain carries you."

Baek Cheon stared at the emaciated boy in stunned, tearful silence. His knees locked. With a ragged, guttural roar that echoed across the Shaanxi canyon, he forced his trembling legs forward, hauling the granite boulder into the fading light.
