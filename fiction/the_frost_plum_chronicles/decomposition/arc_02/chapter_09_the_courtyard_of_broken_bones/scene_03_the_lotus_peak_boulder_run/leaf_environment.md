Sunset ignited the jagged crags of Mount Hua into fangs of burning copper and blood-crimson. Below the precipice, the autumn plains of Shaanxi stretched into freezing violet shadows, while the gale howling up the canyon whipped sweat from the disciples' brows and froze it into brittle rime against their temples. The three thousand granite stairs climbed into the burning clouds like an executioner's road, stained with drops of sweat, torn shoe leather, and the sour bile of exhausted boys pushed past every human limit.
