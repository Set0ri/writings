Dawn broke over Lotus Blossom Peak in shades of bruised amethyst and biting frost. Thick white mountain mist curled across the fractured quartz flagstones of the upper sparring yard, smelling of wet cedar boughs, cold granite, and cheap pine incense. The temperature hovered barely above freezing, pearling heavy dewdrops onto the weapons racks. In the center of the courtyard, twelve first-class disciples stood in two pristine lines, dressed in spotless white-and-black robes with silk lapels that flapped in the brisk canyon wind. Their slender steel swords sliced the morning fog with high, decorative swishes that sounded pretty and threatened absolutely no one.
