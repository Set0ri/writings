### The Hollow Poise of Baek Cheon
**Composite Council Score:** 0.946

---

Dawn broke over Lotus Blossom Peak in shades of bruised amethyst and biting frost. Thick white mountain mist curled across the fractured quartz flagstones of the upper sparring yard, smelling of wet cedar boughs, cold granite, and cheap pine incense. The temperature hovered barely above freezing, pearling heavy dewdrops onto the weapons racks. In the center of the courtyard, twelve first-class disciples stood in two pristine lines, dressed in spotless white-and-black robes with silk lapels that flapped in the brisk canyon wind. Their slender steel swords sliced the morning fog with high, decorative swishes that sounded pretty and threatened absolutely no one.

Baek Cheon, the prized prodigy of the first-class disciples, drifted through the sword forms with the effortless poise of a court aristocrat. His silk sleeves billowed like swan wings; his slender sword cut five graceful petal arcs in the dewy air, concluding with a dramatic spin on one toe and a solemn gaze toward the rising sun. Every posture was geometrically immaculate for a painting, but biologically hollow: his elbow was cocked fifteen degrees too high, his weight was pitched onto his decorative toes rather than his rooted dantian, and the entire sequence produced barely enough kinetic force to slice a ripe persimmon. Behind him, perched atop the rotting wooden fence with his sandals dangling, Jin chewed on a bitter grass stalk, his left eye twitching with homicidal irritation.

*"PFFFFFFFFFT!"*

The sound was so loud, wet, and utterly disrespectful that Baek Cheon's blade froze mid-flourish. The entire courtyard turned in paralyzed shock toward the fence rail, where Jin had just spat his chewed grass stalk into a puddle.

"What on earth was that?" Jin yelled, hopping off the rail and slapping his knees in deranged laughter. "Are you performing at a noble lady's birthday banquet?! Look at those hips! Waving that sword around like a pregnant duck flapping its wings in an autumn bog! Hey! Pretty-boy Dong-ryong! Did you spend three hours in front of the bronze mirror this morning just to swing like a sick maiden?!"

Baek Cheon's face flushed from porcelain pale to violent crimson, a thick blue vein throbbing wildly against his temple. "You... you insolent third-class beggar! How dare you utter that ridiculous nickname in the sacred courtyard?! What right does a fresh novice have to evaluate the sword of Mount Hua's vanguard?!"

"The sword of Mount Hua?" Jin's wolf-smile vanished, replaced by an expression of such cold, lethal contempt that the temperature on the peak seemed to plunge ten degrees. "That toy in your hand isn't Mount Hua's sword. It's a chicken feather. And I'm about to pluck you clean."
