### The Pillaged Pavilion of Scriptures
**Composite Council Score:** 0.942

---

Silence filled the Pavilion of Scriptures like cold water filling a sunken crypt. Slanted afternoon sunbeams cut through torn paper lattices, illuminating millions of floating dust motes dancing over forty linear meters of empty cedar shelving. The air was thick with the scent of aged cedar resin, desiccated spiderwebs, and the pale, lingering phantom of ink long since decomposed. Every mahogany rack—carved by master artisans with the sacred five-petal plum crest—stood hollow and violated, like ribcages stripped clean by scavengers. Even the ornamental brass drawer pulls had been ruthlessly pried away with iron chisels to be pawned for scrap metal.

Jin ran his trembling, calloused fingertips along the empty mahogany mortises. The dark dust outlines revealed the exact dimensions of eight-by-two-inch cylindrical jade scroll tubes—the ancient repository of Mount Hua's high-tier sword arts. Heavy chisel gouges scarred the wood around the lock plates, evidence of brutal, hurried theft. Jin’s thumb paused over a faded silk label pinned to an empty cubby: *The Falling Plum Thirty-Six Movements*. Beneath his touch, the cedar groaned as a faint tremor of involuntary Qi escaped his fingers, spiderwebbing the dry shelf with hair-thin fractures.

"During the Great Drought two centuries ago," Yoon Jong whispered from the doorway, his shoulders slumped in generational shame, "the elders were forced to pawn the lower manuals to buy millet for the starving orphans. Then, when the debts compounded, the Southern Edge Sect marched onto Lotus Peak with imperial bailiffs and confiscated the core scripture chests as 'indemnification.' They left us with nothing but empty shelves and a name that everyone mocks."

"Indemnification?" Jin turned, his wolf-eyes burning with a cold, terrifying fire that made Yoon Jong reflexively step backward. "Southern Edge didn't just take manuals, Yoon Jong. Their famed 'Seventeen Wave Sword' is nothing more than our basic Third Plum Blossom form with the blade flipped upside down! Those shameless mountain thieves copied our ancestors' homework and didn't even have the brains to scrub off the original seal!"

He took a slow, deep breath that whistled through his teeth like winter frost. "They took paper. But paper is only ink and dead wood. The true sword of Mount Hua is not carved on bamboo, boy. It is carved into the bone."
