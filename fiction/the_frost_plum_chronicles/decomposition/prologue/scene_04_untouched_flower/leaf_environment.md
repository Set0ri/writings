Yet inside the two-inch eye of the kinetic storm, the fragile mountain flower trembled in cool, undisturbed stillness. Its translucent petals held a single bead of dew, catching the sudden sunlight like a diamond dropped in ash.

The low acoustic trumpet blast of the Partner Elephant rolled across the ranks, displacing the sulfuric stench with a wave of compressed mountain ozone.
