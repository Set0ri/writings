# Leaf Node: Actions (A*)
**Scene:** The Subterranean Impact & Untouched Flower
**Council Score:** Total 0.964 (Canon: 0.98, Kinetics: 0.98, Sensory: 0.95, Feral: 0.94)

---

*BOOM.*

Jin struck the frozen clay at the foot of Mount Kailash.

Upon impact, he did not absorb the force; he directed it. He drove ninety-nine percent of his kinetic energy into a subterranean forty-five-degree cone deep into the Himalayan bedrock. The earth blasted outward in a vitrified ring forty paces across, the clay flash-fusing into obsidian slag under the incomprehensible pressure.

Yet two inches from his left boot, trapped in the dead-zone vacuum pocket he had deliberately carved with a counter-vortex of plum-blossom Qi, stood a single mountain flower. Its pale pink petals trembled in the backdraft. Not a leaf was torn. Not a speck of soot soiled its delicate stem.

A deafening, reverberating bellow split the mountain air.

The Queen’s Partner Elephant reared back on its hind legs, its etched copper trunk raised toward the sky. It released a massive, low-frequency acoustic trumpet blast. The shockwave surged across the valley floor, blowing away the towering wall of impact dust and revealing Jin kneeling in the vitrified center.
