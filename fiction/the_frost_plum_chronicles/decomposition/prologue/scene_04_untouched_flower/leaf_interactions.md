# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The Subterranean Impact & Untouched Flower
**Council Score:** Total 0.956 (Canon: 0.97, Kinetics: 0.94, Sensory: 0.96, Feral: 0.95)

---

The conscripted Mount Hua disciples massed in the vanguard ranks stared in stunned disbelief, their mud-caked spears trembling in their hands.

*"Elder Brother...?"* a skinny apprentice gasped, clutching a splintered pine training sword against his chest, tears cutting pale furrows through the grime on his cheeks. *"It's him... Elder Brother Jin really jumped from the heavens!"*

In the center of the crater, Jin rose slowly to his full height. Blood trickled from his nostrils and the corners of his mouth where internal organs had bruised against the decelerative shock, but his spine was straight as an ancient cedar.

He did not look at the army. He looked down at the tiny pink mountain flower trembling two inches from his left boot, its delicate petals untouched by soot or fire.

*"Still blooming,"* Jin muttered under his breath, a faint, dangerous smirk touching his cracked lips. *"Good."*
