# Leaf Node: Actions (A*)
**Scene:** Southfall Rebirth: The Dried Anchovy & The Oak Stick
**Council Score:** Total 0.968 (Canon: 0.98, Kinetics: 0.97, Sensory: 0.94, Feral: 0.98)

Jin crawled toward the hearth on hands and knees, cursing the heavens with every shallow breath.

Beside the cold stone hearth lay a seasoned winter oak branch, roughly three feet long, knotted and gnarled, used for stoking firewood. 

Jin’s bony fingers closed around the rough bark. 

The balance was abominable. The wood was front-heavy and rough enough to give him splinters. But as his calloused memory settled into the grain, the trembling in his limbs stopped. He rose to his feet, testing the branch with a sharp, two-handed swish through the chilly air:

*WHOOSH.*

A sharp cramp shot through his right rotator cuff. Jin winced, spat onto the dirt, and laughed like a hyena.

*"Good enough to beat an unfilial dog."*

With one barefoot stomp, Jin kicked the flimsy paper door straight off its rusted leather hinges.

*BAM!*

The paper door blew outward into the snowy courtyard. Yeon—a scrawny, wide-eyed girl clutching an empty wicker basket—let out a terrified squeak and fainted dead away, tumbling backward into a pile of winter cabbages.

Uncle Kang, fat and clad in greasy rabbit-fur, jumped three feet in the air, dropping his abacus into the snow.
