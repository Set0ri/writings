# Leaf Node: Environment (E*)
**Scene:** Southfall Rebirth: The Dried Anchovy & The Oak Stick
**Council Score:** Total 0.956 (Canon: 0.96, Kinetics: 0.94, Sensory: 0.98, Feral: 0.94)

The small courtyard of the ancestral Southfall cottage was a squalid portrait of rural neglect: late winter slush, rotting wattle fences, and chickens pecking hopelessly at frozen cabbage stalks. 

The air was bitterly cold, smelling of melting snow, damp firewood smoke, and cold barley gruel. From the eaves, the rhythmic drip-drip-drip of snow-melt struck the icy stones, counting down the seconds until the world realized who had returned.

In the center of the slush, surrounded by rolling abacus beads and cabbage leaves, stood the dried anchovy boy with an oak branch in his hands, his wild hair whipping in the mountain wind, his terrifying wolf-grin defying the five thousand years of silence.
