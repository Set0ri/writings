### VII. The Trishula Sacrifice & Kamina's Wolf-Grin

The air was torn by the double scent of mortal sacrifice and synthetic ruination: steaming human blood smelling of iron and sweat, mingling with the acrid, chemical reek of black coolant hissing against the glacial stones.

The severed obsidian arm struck the shale forty paces away, its clawed fingers twitching rhythmically as internal circuits shorted out in showers of violet spark. 

Beneath the towering shadow of Kailash, Kamina stood impaled, his blood painting the snow, yet his dying wolf-grin shone with the blazing, unbearable brilliance of the rising sun.

The Demon King speed-blitzed forward to obliterate the kneeling Queen and crush the gravity corridor. His void blade—a jagged shard of pure entropy—tore through the fabric of space.

A red flash fell from the celestial slipstream.

*SHINK.*

Kamina intercepted the blow. The black blade pierced him cleanly through the chest, the entropic edge protruding through his shoulder blade in a hiss of boiling blood.

The Demon King froze, expecting the mortal to dissolve into ash.

Instead, Kamina’s bloodied hands snapped shut around the Demon King’s wrist like iron manacles. Blood welled between his teeth, but his eyes blazed with an unhinged, glorious, terrifying wolf-grin.

*"Did you really think..."* Kamina coughed, laughing with ragged, defiant joy, *"that you could kill a man whose heart already beats in tomorrow?!"*

Beneath them, the Demon King’s visor cracked under the terrible glare of Kamina's conviction.

*"Jin!"* Kamina bellowed, his voice echoing across the cosmos. *"The key... is yours! DON'T YOU DARE LOOK BACK!"*

Even with the entropic blade through his vitals, Kamina did not yield an inch of ground. 

Gripping Shiva's relic Trishula with his right hand, he planted his boots into the frozen clay, locking his skeletal frame against the Demon King's weight. He drew upon the very last ember of his living spirit—not the calculated Qi of a scholar, but the wild, blazing fire of the Mount Hua vanguard.

The three bronze prongs of the Trishula ignited with incandescent solar plasma.

With a feral, blood-drenched roar, Kamina wrenched the relic upward in a vicious diagonal sweep.

*RRRRIP!*

The divine prongs sheared cleanly through the Demon King’s left shoulder joint, cleaving through obsidian plating, superconducting tendon-cables, and dark Qi matrices. The Demon King’s left arm was severed whole, spinning through the freezing air in a violent geyser of black synthetic coolant and corrupted blood!

Kamina hurled the Trishula upward into the cerulean slipstream toward Jin's waiting grasp.
