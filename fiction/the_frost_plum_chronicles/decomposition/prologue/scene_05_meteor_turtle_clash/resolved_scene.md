### V. The Grounded Giant Turtle & Demon King Clash

The atmosphere above Kurukshetra turned the color of bruised obsidian and molten bronze. 

The collision of the celestial turtle's thermal plating against the Demon King's dark vortex generated an acoustic standing wave that shattered the hanging icicles of Mount Kailash into a blinding shower of diamond needles. 

The smell was unbearable: superheated titanium, scorched sulfur, and the sour reek of ozone mingling with the stench of sweat and terror from thirty thousand mortal soldiers huddled in the freezing mud below. Violet arc lightning crawled across the ridges like glowing centipedes, illuminating the terrifying silhouette of the Demon King holding back the weight of the heavens.

High on his pillar of jagged slate, the Heavenly Demon King looked down upon the crater. Black fire curled from the seams of his hollow obsidian armor, smelling of sulfur and dead stars.

*"Fleeting vermin,"* the Demon King murmured, his voice echoing not through air, but directly through the marrow of every living bone in the valley. *"Still clinging to mud. Still pretending that suffering has meaning."*

Jin wiped the smear of blood from his jaw with the back of his sleeve, his wolf-eyes narrowing into two slivers of glacial ice.

*"Meaning?"* Jin rasped, his voice low, rasping, and dripping with feral venom. *"Who gave you permission to preach about meaning, you walking scrap heap? My sect didn't climb these mountains to listen to a funeral dirge from a corpse in iron pajamas!"*

A terrifying, rhythmic thrumming shook the stratosphere above them.

Tearing through the upper atmosphere came what appeared to be a secondary falling meteor—an incandescent boulder of burning alloy and ablative plasma screaming toward the valley floor.

It was not a meteor. 

It was the colossal, burning fore-leg of the celestial Giant Turtle Mech-Ship, jettisoned from the orbital berths of the *Hummingbird* to anchor the planetary defense perimeter!

The Heavenly Demon King did not flinch. He blurred from the slate pillar in a streak of abyssal black lightning. 

Speed-blitzing straight into the descending path of the multi-ton celestial foot, the Demon King thrust a single, clawed obsidian gauntlet upward into the sky. A dark vortex of inverted, entropic Qi detonated around his arm, warping ambient light into an event horizon.

*CLASH.*

The Demon King caught the incandescent mechanical talon with one hand in mid-air!

The kinetic backlash rippled across Annapurna. Mountain ridges cracked like brittle pine needles. An anti-gravity pulse detonated outward, lifting thousand-ton boulders into the sky before crushing them into grey powder.
