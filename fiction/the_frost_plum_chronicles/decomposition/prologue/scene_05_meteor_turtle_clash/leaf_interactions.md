# Leaf Node: Interactions (I*)
**Scene:** The Grounded Giant Turtle & Demon King Clash
**Council Score:** Total 0.95 (Canon: 0.97, Kinetics: 0.92, Sensory: 0.94, Feral: 0.97)

High on his pillar of jagged slate, the Heavenly Demon King looked down upon the crater. Black fire curled from the seams of his hollow obsidian armor, smelling of sulfur and dead stars.

*"Fleeting vermin,"* the Demon King murmured, his voice echoing not through air, but directly through the marrow of every living bone in the valley. *"Still clinging to mud. Still pretending that suffering has meaning."*

Jin wiped the smear of blood from his jaw with the back of his sleeve, his wolf-eyes narrowing into two slivers of glacial ice.

*"Meaning?"* Jin rasped, his voice low, rasping, and dripping with feral venom. *"Who gave you permission to preach about meaning, you walking scrap heap? My sect didn't climb these mountains to listen to a funeral dirge from a corpse in iron pajamas!"*

A terrifying, rhythmic thrumming shook the stratosphere above them.
