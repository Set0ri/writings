The atmosphere burned from indigo vacuum to incandescent scarlet as seven thousand degrees of friction wrapped Jin's descending silhouette in an atmospheric meteor sleeve. 

Below him, the storm shelf across Annapurna stretched like an iron roof forty leagues wide, bloated with hail and black lightning. 

When Jin's feet-first spear pierced the clouds, the detonation was instantaneous. The shockwave stripped the cloud ceiling clean to the horizon, vaporizing millions of tons of moisture into dry, shimmering ion-mist. In an eyeblink, the dreary gloom of Kurukshetra vanished, replaced by the stark, razor-sharp glare of Himalayan winter sunlight reflecting off glaciers of ancient ice.
