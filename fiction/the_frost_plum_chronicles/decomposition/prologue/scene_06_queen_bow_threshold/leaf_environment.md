The battlefield fell into an uncanny, terrifying silence. 

The low acoustic rumble of the flying Tech-Whale seemed to mute itself before the spectacle of the Empress kneeling in the mud. The earth was freezing, smelling of overturned clay, spilled blood, and bitter pine sap. Thirty thousand conscripts stood like statues of stone, tears freezing against their bearded cheeks as they watched their divine ruler eating the dirt of Kurukshetra to buy them breath.

Above her bowed back, the cerulean gravity corridor hummed, a ribbon of pure, frictionless blue light spiraling straight up into the dark heavens.
