Framed against the blinding, white-hot solar flares of the sun ninety million miles away, the Solar Lotus Dyson mandala hung in cosmic space, its crystalline petals holding three full cycles of solar plasma in magnetic containment, waiting for the dual keys.

The vacuum was utterly silent, yet the warm titanium plumage beneath their boots pulsed with the steady, thrumming heartbeat of the ship's antimatter core. Below them, Earth was an fragile marble of swirl and sapphire, wrapped in the dark storm cloud gathering over Annapurna.
