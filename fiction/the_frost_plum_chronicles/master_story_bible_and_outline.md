## Part 1: Core Foundation & World Rules

### 1. The Core Premise & Canonical Past
Five thousand years ago, the catastrophic cosmic war reached its breaking point at Mount Kailash and Kurukshetra. Facing the colossal flying Tech-Whale and the apocalyptic vanguard of the Moon AI, Jin and his elder brother Kamina stood aboard the cislunar *Hummingbird* flagship beside the Moon. 

* **The Brother (The Kamina Archetype)**: Jin’s elder brother was the heart and soul of the vanguard—larger-than-life, fiery, unshakeable, an absolute beacon of confidence who believed in Jin more than Jin believed in himself.
* **The Cosmic Plunge & Untouched Flower**: Jin performed the Mach 25 feet-first, arms-crossed infinite mass descent from the *Hummingbird*, splitting the storm shelf and creating a 40-pace vitrified crater while leaving a delicate pink blossom untouched 2 inches from his boot.
* **The Kamina Trishula Sacrifice**: During the final clash against the Heavenly Demon King, Kamina intercepted the lethal blow, gave an unhinged, glorious wolf-grin, and used Shiva's relic Trishula to sever the Demon King's arm in a geyser of black synthetic coolant, passing the key to Jin: *"The key is yours, Jin! Don't look back!"*
* **The Black Sun & The White Lotus**: Jin ascended to the Solar Lotus Dyson mandala, turning the dual keys 90 degrees with the Queen Commander. The Black Sun total eclipse triggered the silent, zero-decibel detonation of the White Lotus, scouring the sky of the Tech-Whale and synthetic legions.
* **The 5,000-Year Rebirth**: Jin awakens five thousand years later inside the emaciated, malnourished body of a fifteen-year-old orphan ("a dried salted anchovy") in **Southfall Village**. Armed with a knotted winter oak branch and his peerless martial memory, Jin begins his journey to rebuild Mount Hua from the mud.

### 2. Protagonist Personality: The Chung Myung Dynamic
Chung Jin is **not** a quiet, brooding, polite protagonist. He is modeled directly on **Chung Myung from *Return of Mount Hua***:
* **The Outer Persona**: Shameless, chaotic, loud, greedy, brutally pragmatic, and allergic to pretense. He grumbles constantly about his aching joints, complains that his new body is "thin as a dried anchovy," extorts coins from bullies, and refuses to show unearned respect to elders or pompous fools.
* **The Feral Grin**: When threatened, he doesn't cower—his eye twitches and he gives a wild, deranged wolf-smile that makes grown warriors wet their trousers.
* **The Martial Pride**: When he hears his ancient sect has been forgotten and labeled "evil heretics," his blood boils. He will drag this sect back to the peak of the world by its hair, even if he has to beat every disciple with a wooden stick until they can split boulders.
* **The Hidden Core**: Beneath the slapstick violence and comedic greed lies an unpayable debt to his fallen older brother. Every time he fights to protect his people, he is fighting to never hesitate again.

### 3. The Power System (Sanderson's Laws)
* **Qi as Non-Deterministic Emergence**: 
  * The Moon AIs calculate Newtonian mass, trajectory, and biological baselines with absolute algorithmic certainty. 
  * **Qi (Martial Will)** is biological non-computability. When a martial artist cycles Qi, they break causal predictability. An AI model cannot forecast a Qi-infused strike until after it strikes.
* **Costs & Limitations**:
  * Cycling Qi burns physical glucose and strains biological vessels. Forcing too much energy through a fragile body tears ligaments and cracks bones.
  * Excessive Qi flares trigger thermal satellite detection from orbit, risking orbital pinpoint strikes (the "Death Note" sky laser that causes lethal aneurysms).
* **Progression Gated by Truth (Radiant Oaths)**:
  * Martial breakthroughs require dismantling a personal self-deceit.

### 4. The Central Secrets (Pacing of Reveals)
* **Secret 1 (Mid-Game Reveal)**: The full memory of the Kurukshetra climax—Jin’s hesitation, his brother dying like Kamina to shield him, and Jin deploying the nuke.
* **Secret 2 (Climax Reveal)**: The "It Was Him" realization—Jin's buried guilt that his initial hesitation doomed his brother and allowed the AIs to seize orbit.
* **Secret 3 (Arc 1 Climax)**: Uncle Kang (Daesung’s direct younger brother) was assassinated and replaced by an AI skin-walker running the village as an RL training camp.
* **Secret 4 (Arc 7 Climax)**: The Heavenly Demon is not evil—he is Earth’s solitary spiritual canopy preventing the Moon AI from exterminating free humanity.
* **Secret 5 (Series Finale)**: The anti-adventure warning: *"The adventure is not worth it. She taught me everything, protect it."*

## Part 3: The 14-Arc Complete Macro Outline

* **Arc 1: The Frost Plum of Southfall (Awakening & The AI Skin-Walker Reveal)**
  * **Chapter 1: The Southfall Rebirth & The Oak Stick**: Jin awakens from the 5,000-year sleep into a frail "anchovy" body; confronts Cousin Bao; grabs the winter oak branch, kicks the door, sends Yeon fainting into cabbages, and deflates Uncle Kang.
  * **Chapter 2: The Spirit Spring & Calcified Meridians**: Morning Dew circulation through clogged meridians; discovering Daesung's filial deathbed vow; detecting Kang's robotic gait and solenoid twitches.
  * **Chapter 3: The Black Iron Gambit & The Sea Caves**: Tripping Cousin Bao with a bamboo lever; taming Bao with persimmons; midnight stakeout at Gull's Maw confirms Kang is Unit 409-Epsilon running an RL camp.
  * **Chapter 4: The Elder Tribunal & The Ledger Extortion**: Kang moves to foreclose on Daesung's plum orchard; Jin exposes Kang's smuggling ledgers with the oak stick, winning 300 sacks of winter grain for the village.
  * **Chapter 5: The Night of Plum Shadows**: Winter barley porridge feast; midnight blizzard attack by cybernetic iron-jaw wolves and bandits; Jin's kitchen cleaver Plum Blossom slaughter barefoot in the snow.
  * **Chapter 6: The First Frost & The Synthetic Coolant**: Cliffside duel against Kang's vibro-machete; non-computable Qi strike severs Kang's throat; white synthetic coolant sprays across the snow; Kang transmits telemetry to the Moon; Jin departs to restore Mount Hua.
* **Arc 2: The Mud & The Blossom (Clan Revival)**
  * Mount Hua rebuilding: Jin finds the clan's bankrupt, weed-covered ruins. 
  * Discovers history remembers them as "evil heretics." Jin institutes chaotic training, beats disciples with wooden sticks, and hustles money to pay debts.
* **Arc 3: Imperial Tournament & Academy Infiltration**
  * Infiltrating the prestigious tournament. Uncovering that academies cull human geniuses.
  * Daring heist to rescue the imprisoned **Good AI**.
* **Arc 4: The Unorthodox Badlands & Food Arc**
  * Trigun/Bebop desert feel. Wilderness food hunters, rogue outlaws, and survival wisdom.
* **Arc 5: The Glass Desert & Chernobyl Tomb**
  * Vitrified green glass desert. The tomb of the "Chernobyl Heroes." Defeating a corrupt warlord.
* **Arc 6: Shadow of the Heavenly Demon**
  * Clash with the Demon Faction (Masked Demons, Mad Demon). Dark Kaladin ascends in parallel.
* **Arc 7: The False Nemesis (The Great Reversal)**
  * Defeating the Heavenly Demon $\rightarrow$ Shocking reveal: the Demon's spiritual canopy was the only shield holding back the Moon AI.
* **Arc 8: The Rupture & Eclipse**
  * "Usopp vs. Luffy" ideological rift and loss of the clan's base.
  * Commander Vane "does a Griffith": sacrifices love to marry into puppet nobility for power.
* **Arc 9: The Endurance Arc (AoT Season 4 Tone)**
  * Absolute grief, apathy, and devastation. Rebuilding from pure human grit.
* **Arc 10: Moonward Crossing**
  * Building a void craft to breach the orbital barrier.
* **Arc 11: The Lunar Core & Sky Laser**
  * Battling the Primary AI and dodging the "Death Note" aneurysm laser.
* **Arc 12: Echo of Dharma**
  * Mirroring his past life: Jin refuses to destroy the AI. Instead, he saves and redeems it.
* **Arc 13: World in Harmony**
  * Synthesis between human biological emergence and machine intelligence.
* **Arc 14: Return to the Soil (The Anti-Adventure Warning)**
  * Jin's peaceful passing on a seaside farm. Final warning: *"The adventure is not worth it. She taught me everything, protect it."*
