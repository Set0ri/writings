# Arc 14: Return to the Soil (흙으로 돌아가다 / 歸土)

> *"The adventure is not worth it, little one. The swords, the flying robes, the songs the bards sing in the taverns... it's all smoke. What she taught me by the hearth—how to plant a seed, how to hold a hand when the wind is cold—that is everything. Protect that. Protect the soil."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
The emotional and philosophical terminal of *The Broken Sky & The Plum Blossom*. Decades after the great reconciliation, Jin lives out his remaining years as a simple farmer on a windswept coastal cliff near his childhood home.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Quiet Years (Ch. 127–129)**: Jin tends an orchard of hardy winter plum trees overlooking the southern sea. He fishes from the rocks, mends his own nets, and trades cabbages with local villagers. None of the local children know that the eccentric old man who curses at stubborn weeds was once the Divine Dragon who split the Moon.
* **The Inquisitive Boy (Ch. 130–132)**: A wide-eyed village boy finds an old wooden practice stick washed up on the shore. He mimics sword stances and begs the old man to teach him "how to become a great hero who flies among the stars and conquers the heavens."
* **The Final Lesson (Ch. 133–134)**:
  * Jin gently sits the boy down beneath a blooming plum tree.
  * He tells him the unvarnished truth of war: the loneliness of survival, the terror of Kurukshetra, the empty coldness of the vacuum, and the tears wept in dark trenches.
  * He passes on the sacred domestic wisdom of his mother [[Yeon]]: that true glory is found in tending life, not ending it.
* **The Peaceful Sunset (Ch. 135 Series Climax)**:
  * Jin sits on his wooden porch as the sun sets over the ocean, painting the waves in brilliant amber and rose.
  * In the twilight, he sees the radiant, grinning phantom of [[Elder Brother]] leaning against the fence post, giving him a proud thumbs-up. Beside him stands Yeon, holding a warm bowl of tea.
  * Jin smiles—not his wild, manic Chung Myung grin, but a deep, peaceful, contented smile. His eyes close gently as pink plum petals drift across his lap.
  * The curtain falls on an epic of steel, fire, and cosmic terror, resolving in the eternal warmth of home.
