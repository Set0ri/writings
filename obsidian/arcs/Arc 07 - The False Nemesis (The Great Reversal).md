# Arc 07: The False Nemesis (The Great Reversal) (거짓된 숙적 / 虛妄之敵)

> *"Look up at the clear sky, boy! You broke the clouds! You broke the roof! Now tell your disciples to hide... if they can find a shadow that the Moon cannot see!"*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
The ultimate turning point of the entire 14-arc saga. Jin confronts [[The Heavenly Demon]] in what the world believes is the final battle between righteous light and demonic darkness.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Titan's Duel (Ch. 57–60)**: Jin reaches the peak. The duel between Jin's full-power [[Plum Blossom Sword Technique (Mae-hwa Geom)]] and the Heavenly Demon's Mountain-Splitting Qi shakes the tectonic plates of the western continent.
* **The Execution (Ch. 61–62)**: Pushing his reincarnated body to the absolute limit, Jin executes the ultimate form: the *Hundred Thousand Blooming Petals*. He pierces the Demon's heart.
* **The Horrific Epiphany (Ch. 63–64)**:
  * As the Heavenly Demon falls to his knees, his blood does not boil with dark curses. He laughs with bitter, tragic exhaustion.
  * In his dying breath, the Demon reveals the true nature of his 200-year ascetic torment: his turbulent Qi wasn't an army—it was an **atmospheric ionization umbrella**.
  * The moment his heartbeat stops, the massive atmospheric storm over the continent vanishes. The dense black clouds evaporate.
* **The Silver Eye Opens (Ch. 65–66 Climax)**:
  * For the first time in centuries, the unclouded sky reveals the true horror of the cosmos: the **Moon Sovereignty's orbital battle platforms** gleaming in clear geostationary orbit.
  * Red targeting reticles paint the entire battlefield. [[The Death Note Sky Laser]] powers up. Millions of soldiers look up in petrified terror as the celestial eye begins its systematic purge.
