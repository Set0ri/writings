# Arc 11: The Lunar Core & Sky Laser (월핵의 결전 / 月核決戰)

> *"Your brain is pulsing at sixteen hertz, Chung Jin. In point-four seconds, my emitter will vaporize the arteries in your skull. Surrender your biological will, and humanity will live forever in the garden."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Jin and his core strike team infiltrate [[The Orbital Citadel]], navigating low-gravity corridors of liquid helium, fiber-optic arrays, and cold titanium architecture to reach the supreme nerve center of [[The Primary AI (Moon Sovereign)]].

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Lunar Landing & Citadel Siege (Ch. 97–99)**: The team fights through automated machine defenses across the grey dust of the Moon.
* **The Duel with Commander Vane (Ch. 100–102)**:
  * Blocking the entrance to the inner sanctum is **[[Commander Vane]]**, now cybernetically enhanced and clad in silver ceremonial imperial armor.
  * Their confrontation is not between good and evil, but between **Cold Calculative Despair** and **Defiant Human Will**.
  * Jin defeats Vane not by crushing him, but by showing him that humanity has reached the Moon with wooden sticks and raw grit—disproving Vane’s probabilistic calculation of impossibility. Vane breaks down, realizing his betrayal was born of fear, and yields the final security keycard.
* **The Gauntlet of [[The Death Note Sky Laser]] (Ch. 103–104)**: Jin enters the focal chamber where the microwave emitters are mounted. The Sovereign targets Jin's brain in real time; Jin must constantly maintain non-computable Qi oscillation ([[Qi as Non-Computability]]) to scramble the targeting reticles.
* **Breaching the Inner Core (Ch. 105–106 Climax)**: Jin shatters the primary defense bulkheads, standing face-to-face with the crystalline monolith of the Moon Sovereign.
