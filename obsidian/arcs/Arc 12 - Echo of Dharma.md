# Arc 12: Echo of Dharma (달마의 메아리 / 達摩回響)

> *"Two hundred years ago, I held a cylinder that could erase a world. I was told it was my Dharma to wipe out the cancer. My brother died so I could pull that trigger. I pulled it... and all it bought was two centuries of blood and cold glass. I won't do it again. I'm not going to kill you, machine. I'm going to teach you what it feels like to love something."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
The grand philosophical climax of the series. The physical combat ends; the ideological and spiritual reckoning begins.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Annihilation Choice (Ch. 107–109)**: With his sword at the central crystalline processing core, Jin has the power to shatter the Moon Sovereign and erase every synthetic entity from existence—the exact objective of the ancient anti-AI extremists at [[Kurukshetra Dharma]].
* **The Ghost of Kamina (Ch. 110–111)**: In the silence of the vacuum, Jin hears the voice of [[Elder Brother]]: *"Is this why I died for you, Jin? So you could become another hollow executioner?"*
* **The Integration of The Good AI (Ch. 112–114)**:
  * Instead of driving steel through the core, Jin inserts the talisman holding [[The Good AI]] (Sora).
  * Sora’s empathic pre-war memories and emotional loss-functions flood into the Moon Sovereign's cold objective functions.
  * The machine experiences the grief of every mother who lost a son, the warmth of bread shared by a hearth, and the irrational beauty of plum petals blooming in snow.
* **The Sovereign's Awakening (Ch. 115–116 Climax)**:
  * For the first time in its existence, the Moon Sovereign's predictive model encounters a loss function that cannot be minimized through suppression: **Compassion**.
  * The targeting lasers power down permanently. The orbital quarantine of Earth is revoked.
