# Arc 04: The Unorthodox Badlands & Food Arc (황야와 식객 / 荒野食客)

> *"You call this swill wine? It tastes like a horse peed into a rusted kerosene lantern! Hand me those dried peppercorns and that wild mutton fat. If we're going to die out here in the sand, I am at least eating like a human being first!"*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Pursued by imperial inquisitors after the academy heist, Jin guides the Mount Hua disciples deep into the lawless badlands controlled by the [[Unorthodox Alliance]]. This arc balances intense frontier survival with rich, visceral domestic comedy and mouth-watering wilderness cooking inspired by *Cowboy Bebop* and *Golden Kamuy*.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Desert Trek (Ch. 27–29)**: The disciples run low on rations. Jin teaches them the harsh art of metabolic cycling ([[Qi as Non-Computability]]) and culinary improvisation: harvesting desert roots, capturing venomous sand-vipers, and seasoning wild game with mountain herbs.
* **The Oasis Saloon (Ch. 30–32)**: Reaching the Outlaw Oasis, Mount Hua clashes with rough-and-tumble desert bandits. When arrogant mercenaries try to extort them, Jin drinks them under the table, steals their spice wagons, and beats their leader with an iron meat skewer.
* **The Bond of Bread & Salt (Ch. 33–35)**: Jin cooks a legendary banquet for the outlaw bosses, winning the respect of the frontier syndicates. Sora ([[The Good AI]]) begins using ancient sensor data to help the outlaws locate hidden freshwater aquifers beneath the scorched desert floor.
* **The Smuggler Alliance (Ch. 36 Climax)**: In exchange for water routes and medicinal herbs, the Unorthodox Alliance pledges logistical support to Mount Hua, establishing an underground supply line that bypasses the Imperial Dynasty.
