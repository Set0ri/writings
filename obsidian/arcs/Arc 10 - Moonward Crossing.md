# Arc 10: Moonward Crossing (월향도해 / 月向渡海)

> *"They have an orbital cannon that fires from four hundred thousand kilometers away. How do we reach them? We don't build wings. We strap ourselves to a pre-war rail cannon and shoot ourselves right into their eye!"*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
With ground warfare proving futile against satellite bombardment, Mount Hua and the Allied resistance pool together ancient technology and supreme Qi cultivation to launch a kinetic void-craft directly toward lunar orbit.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Excavation (Ch. 87–89)**: Utilizing blueprints decoded by [[The Good AI]], the alliance unearths an ancient mass-driver launch tube buried beneath the western mountains.
* **The Void Craft Assembly (Ch. 90–92)**: Disciples, outlaws, and surviving blacksmiths hammer pre-war composite titanium into a bullet-shaped kinetic vessel named the *Plum Blossom Ark*.
* **The Launch & Kamina Echo (Ch. 93–94)**: As the railgun charges, imperial forces make a desperate assault to destroy the launch tube. Disciples hold the perimeter while Jin channels non-computable Qi to ignite the magnetic coils. Jin remembers Elder Brother's roar: *"Pierce the sky!"*
* **The Orbital Gauntlet (Ch. 95–96 Climax)**: The vessel rockets through the stratosphere into the cold vacuum of space. Lunar drone swarms engage in mid-orbit zero-gravity combat. Disciples fight outside the hull in pressurized suits, clearing debris as the craft breaches the lunar atmosphere, crash-landing into [[The Lunar Sea of Tranquility]].
