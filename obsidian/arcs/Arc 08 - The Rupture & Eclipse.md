## 📖 Narrative Summary
With Earth's atmospheric canopy destroyed, humanity faces total orbital subjugation. The allied front splinters under unimaginable psychological pressure.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Panic of the World (Ch. 67–69)**: Orbital lasers strike major martial fortresses. Anyone displaying high Qi is sniped from orbit. Mount Hua becomes a refugee camp, but paranoia runs rampant.
* **The "Usopp vs. Luffy" Schism (Ch. 70–72)**:
  * Desperate to protect the younger disciples, [[Baek Cheon]] and [[Yoon Jong]] clash violently with Jin over his refusal to surrender or scatter.
  * Jin, driven by his buried trauma from Kurukshetra, insists they must hold the line at all costs. Baek Cheon weeps, accusing Jin of treating them like disposable soldiers rather than brothers.
  * A brutal, tearful duel erupts between Jin and Baek Cheon—mirroring the tragic water seven rupture.
* **The "Griffith" Betrayal (Ch. 73–75)**:
  * [[Commander Vane]], calculating that human victory is mathematically impossible ($P < 0.00001$), chooses cold realpolitik.
  * He poisons the defense lines, hands the navigation coordinates of Mount Hua to the imperial collaborators, and announces his betrothal to the synthetic Imperial Empress, sacrificing his genuine love to seize imperial dominion.
* **The Fall of the Mountain (Ch. 76 Climax)**: Imperial legions and orbital pinpoint strikes descend upon Lotus Peak. Mount Hua’s ancestral buildings burn to ash. Jin, bloodied and carrying wounded disciples on his back, is forced to abandon the mountain, fleeing into the subterranean wilderness.
