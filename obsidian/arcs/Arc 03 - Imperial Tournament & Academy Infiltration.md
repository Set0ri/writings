# Arc 03: Imperial Tournament & Academy Infiltration (학림의 제전 / 學林祭典)

> *"They applaud in their gold-embroidered boxes while boys bleed on the flagstones. They think they are picking generals. But the smell rising from beneath the flagstones isn't incense—it's liquid nitrogen and copper coolant."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Following their victory over Southern Edge, Mount Hua earns an official invitation to the Grand Imperial Martial Tournament held in [[The Imperial Capital]]. Jin suspects the Imperial Academies are actively collaborating with the orbital presence.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Imperial Stage (Ch. 17–19)**: Mount Hua arrives in the glittering capital. The disciples face scorn from royal academies. Jin coaches Baek Cheon and Yu Iseol, guiding them to dominate the preliminary brackets without exposing their highest Qi signatures.
* **Allies & Rivals Introduced (Ch. 20–21)**:
  * Jin crosses paths with **[[Commander Vane]]**, the brilliant young general whose tactical brilliance saves a border garrison from bandit extermination.
  * Jin encounters **[[Dark Kaladin]]** ("The Boy"), an impoverished youth fighting with a vicious spear to win medical subsidies for his dying sister.
* **The Subterranean Infiltration (Ch. 22–24)**: Guided by strange electrical hums beneath the arena, Jin breaks through the drainage sewers into the Imperial University's deepest vault.
  * **The Horror Discovery**: The imperial civil examinations are a culling system. The brightest minds are placed in cryogenic stasis, their brains interconnected into biological neural network clusters to feed data to [[The Primary AI (Moon Sovereign)]].
  * Jin discovers **[[The Good AI]]** (Sora), chained to the cooling towers, weeping data tears as she is forced to coordinate the culling.
* **The Heist & Escape (Ch. 25–26 Climax)**: Jin bypasses the biometric firewalls using high-frequency sword vibrations, extracts Sora's core matrix into a brass jade talisman, and battles imperial inquisitors across the capital's rooftops during the tournament finals. Mount Hua slips away into the night with the greatest secret in the realm.
