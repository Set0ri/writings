# Arc 02: The Mud & The Blossom (진흙과 꽃 / 泥中花)

> *"You call yourselves swordsmen of Mount Hua? You swing your blades like pregnant ducks waving reeds in an autumn swamp! Drop those swords! From today on, every one of you will carry two boulders up Lotus Peak before sunrise. If you faint, I will kick you down the stairs so you can climb up twice!"*
