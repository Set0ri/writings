# Arc 05: The Glass Desert & Chernobyl Tomb (유리 사막과 무덤 / 硝子荒漠)

> *"Underneath this sheet of green glass lie three hundred thousand men who had mothers and dreams. We didn't fight for glory. We fought because madmen told us the sky had to be cleansed. And now look at it. Just cold, silent glass."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Jin leads Mount Hua into the forbidden vitrified wastes—the ground zero of [[Kurukshetra Dharma]] two centuries ago, where Jin deployed [[The Brass Cylinder Weapon]]. The soil has turned into miles of smooth, reflective green obsidian known as [[The Glass Desert]].

### Key Arc Progression:
* **Walking on Ancient Bones (Ch. 37–39)**: Jin walks across the very ground where his past life ended. He struggles to suppress overwhelming panic attacks and auditory hallucinations of artillery thunder. The disciples marvel at the glassy expanse, oblivious that their eccentric junior brother was the one who melted it.
* **The Chernobyl Tomb (Ch. 40–42)**: The group locates a colossal concrete and lead sarcophagus—the tomb erected by ancient engineers to seal the leaking singularity core and toxic isotopic fallout.
  * Inside, they uncover murals and copper plaques honoring the **Chernobyl Heroes**: ancient mortal liquidators who marched into fatal radiation with lead shovels to protect future generations.
  * Jin experiences a profound wave of reverent grief, realizing that true heroes were not the bomb-makers, but the ordinary men who shoveled ash to save life.
* **The Cobalt Warlord Confrontation (Ch. 43–45)**: A rogue military despot has set up camp over the reactor core, draining raw isotopic power to forge radioactive armor and terrorize surrounding settlements.
* **The Clean Strike (Ch. 46 Climax)**: Jin utilizes a purified variant of [[Plum Blossom Sword Technique (Mae-hwa Geom)]] to sever the warlord's isotopic power lines without breaching the sarcophagus, resealing the tomb forever.
