# Arc 09: The Endurance Arc (Ashes of Hua) (인내의 잿더미 / 忍耐之燼)

> *"The mountain is gone. The hall is ash. The swords are broken. Good. Now there is nothing left between us and the dirt. We aren't fighting for a sect anymore, boys. We are fighting because we are still breathing."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Rendered homeless, pursued by imperial hunter-killer squads, and mourning fallen comrades, Mount Hua enters its darkest, most visceral trial—evoking the grim, uncompromising endurance of *Attack on Titan Season 4* and *Vinland Saga*.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Apathy of Defeat (Ch. 77–79)**: Disciples hide in freezing salt mines. Jin sits in catatonic silence, his hands trembling—trapped in a recursive panic loop of his brother’s death at Kurukshetra. The comedic bravado is completely stripped away.
* **Han’s Ascension (Ch. 80–81)**: In Jin’s absence of spirit, **[[Senior Brother Han]]** (the zero-Qi bluffer) steps into true leadership. He tends the frostbite, divides the single turnip among twenty shivering juniors, and stares down an imperial patrol with sheer, cold psychological dominance.
* **The Dismantling of the Lie (Ch. 82–84)**:
  * In a rain-soaked ravine, Baek Cheon finds Jin kneeling before an unlit fire.
  * For the first time in the entire story, Jin confesses the canonical truth of [[Kurukshetra Dharma]]: *"I hesitated. My brother died because I was too weak to pull the trigger. And I burned the world to hide my shame."*
  * Instead of judging him, Baek Cheon, Yu Iseol, and Jo Gul embrace him in the mud. They don't need a legendary saint; they need their broken brother.
* **The Forged Resolve (Ch. 85–86 Climax)**: Purgative rebirth. The disciples forge crude iron blades from mining carts. Mount Hua rises again—not as an ancient name, but as an unbreakable brotherhood ready to take the war to the sky itself.
