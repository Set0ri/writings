# Arc 01: The Frost Plum of Southfall (남하의 설매 / 南下之雪梅)

> *"Five thousand years of sleep, and I wake up in the body of an anchovy. If the gods wanted me dead, they should have let me turn to dust beneath the Black Sun. Since they gave me breath, they'd better start praying."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Chung Jin, the Peerless Sword Saint of Mount Hua, awakens five thousand years after the cosmic catastrophe at [[Kurukshetra Dharma]] and the detonation of the White Lotus. Instead of resting in the void beside Elder Brother Kamina, he finds himself inside the emaciated, malnourished body of a fifteen-year-old orphan in [[Southfall Village]], a remote coastal hamlet.

### Key Chapter Breakdown:
* **Chapter 1**: The Southfall Rebirth & The Oak Stick. Jin awakens from the 5,000-year cosmic sleep into the body of a starving "dried salted anchovy"; confronts [[Cousin Bao]], spotting the unnatural four-finger clamp bruise; grabs the knotted winter oak branch by the hearth, kicks the door off its hinges, sends [[Yeon]] fainting into the cabbages, and puts on a masterclass of Mount Hua lineage discipline before [[Uncle Kang]].
* **Chapter 2**: The Spirit Spring & Calcified Meridians. Morning Dew breathing art; Jin eats dirt and forces a thread of violet Qi through clogged, calcified meridians; discovers [[Daesung]]'s filial deathbed vow; observes Uncle Kang walking the northern palisade with robotic precision (1.150s pace, 0mm variance, unblinking eyes).
* **Chapter 3**: The Black Iron Gambit & The Sea Caves. Jin trips Cousin Bao into pig slurry with a bamboo lever; tames Bao with dried persimmons; tracks Kang to the Gull's Maw sea caves at midnight, witnessing Kang trading grain and iron ore with the [[Black Wave Bandits]] and confirming Southfall is a [[Reinforcement Learning Camps|Reinforcement Learning Camp]] run by an AI skin-walker.
* **Chapter 4**: The Elder Tribunal & The Ledger Extortion. Kang moves to foreclose on Daesung's cottage and ancestral plum orchard; Jin strolls into the tribunal with his knotted oak branch, slams down the stolen smuggling ledgers, breaks Kang's enforcer, and extorts Kang into forgiving all debt and delivering 300 sacks of winter grain.
* **Chapter 5**: The Night of Plum Shadows. The family shares hot barley porridge with sweet plums; at midnight, Kang unleashes cybernetic iron-jaw wolves and Black Wave cutthroats in a blizzard raid; Jin takes two iron kitchen cleavers and executes the Twenty-Four Movement Plum Blossom Sword Art barefoot in the snow, butchering the hounds and raiders.
* **Chapter 6**: The First Frost & The Synthetic Coolant. Jin pursues Kang to the sea cliffs; Kang draws an 8-inch vibro-machete with a 99.98% algorithmic win prediction; Jin executes the non-computable Qi strike, severing Kang's throat; **milky white synthetic coolant** geysers across the snow; Kang transmits combat data to the Moon before dying; Jin bids farewell to Daesung, Yeon, and Bao, setting off for Mount Hua.
