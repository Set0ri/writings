# Arc 01: The Frost Plum of Southfall (남하의 설매 / 南下之雪梅)

> *"Two hundred years of sleep, and I wake up in the body of an anchovy. If the gods wanted me dead, they should have let me turn to dust on the plains. Since they gave me breath, they'd better start praying."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
Chung Jin, the ancient grandmaster of Mount Hua, awakens two hundred years after the cataclysmic Great War at [[Kurukshetra Dharma]]. Instead of arriving in paradise or ascending to the heavens, he finds himself inside the emaciated, malnourished body of a fifteen-year-old boy in [[Southfall Village]], a remote coastal hamlet.

### Key Chapter Breakdown:
* **Chapter 1**: The Kurukshetra Dharma flashback (cuts right before the white flash); Jin wakes up horrified by his twig-like limbs; confrontations with his loud cousin [[Cousin Bao]]; Jin's feral Chung Myung wolf-grin terrifies the village.
* **Chapter 2**: Morning Dew breathing technique; Jin begins rebuilding his shattered meridians; discovers his father [[Daesung]] is bound by a deathbed vow to endure his corrupt younger brother [[Uncle Kang]]; Jin notices Kang's unblinking, mechanical gait.
* **Chapter 3**: Jin ambushes Bao, discovers his ribs are covered in precision bruises inflicted by Kang; tames Bao; midnight stakeout at the sea caves exposes Kang collaborating with the [[Black Wave Bandits]].
* **Chapter 4**: Jin blackmails Uncle Kang with unblinking nerve, demanding 300 sacks of grain and debt forgiveness; Kang's cold, calculating reaction confirms Jin's suspicions.
* **Chapter 5**: The massive grain delivery arrives; family dinner with Daesung and [[Yeon]]; midnight attack—Kang unleashes wolves and bandits to wipe out the village and eliminate witnesses.
* **Chapter 6**: Climax of Arc 1. Jin uses kitchen knives and raw martial leverage to slaughter bandits; confronts Uncle Kang in the sea mist; severs Kang's jugular, revealing **white synthetic coolant** instead of blood. Kang transmits Jin's strike data to the Moon before dying. Jin bids farewell to his family and sets out for the mainland to find Mount Hua.
