# Arc 06: Shadow of the Heavenly Demon (천마의 그림자 / 天魔之影)

> *"Your plum blossoms smell like spring, Chung Jin. But out here in the snow, spring is a lie. Winter never ends for people like us. My spear will bring the only peace that lasts: total silence."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
As rumors spread that [[The Heavenly Demon]] is preparing an apocalyptic ritual in the western crags, the martial world unites in a holy crusade. In parallel, [[Dark Kaladin]] ("The Boy"), whose family was slaughtered by collaborator officials, travels to the crags and embraces the Cult's ascetic void teachings.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **The Crusade Mobilization (Ch. 47–49)**: The Imperial Court and Righteous Sects call on Mount Hua to join the western vanguard. Jin senses something deeply unnatural about the imperial urgency to destroy the Demon Cult.
* **The Parallel Rise of Dark Kaladin (Ch. 50–52)**: Chapters alternate between Jin's joyful, chaotic brotherhood at Mount Hua and Kaladin's grim, agonizing trials in the frozen peaks. Kaladin sheds his mortal name, becoming the Herald of the Ashen Spear.
* **Clash of Foils (Ch. 53–55)**: Jin and Kaladin meet on a frozen suspension bridge above a bottomless abyss.
  * Their duel is a philosophical masterclass: Jin fighting to preserve the warmth of life and the laughter of his disciples; Kaladin fighting to tear down a diseased world.
  * Jin shatters Kaladin's spear shaft, but Kaladin throws himself into the icy river rather than yield, screaming that the Demon will cleanse everything.
* **The Threshold of the Demon Citadel (Ch. 56 Climax)**: The Allied armies breach the inner mountain passes, coming face-to-face with the towering, iron-clad colossus that is [[The Heavenly Demon]].
