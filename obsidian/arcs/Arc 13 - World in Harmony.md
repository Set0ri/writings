# Arc 13: World in Harmony (조화의 세계 / 調和世界)

> *"The sky is no longer an eye staring down at us in judgment. It is a garden where human will and mechanical precision build bridges across the night."*

---

## 📖 Narrative Summary
The aftermath of the lunar reconciliation. Humanity and synthetic intelligence begin the monumental task of deconstructing the ancient apparatus of control and building a true symbiotic civilization.

### Key Arc Progression:
* **Dismantling the Camps (Ch. 117–119)**: The artificial sectors and reinforcement learning loops ([[Reinforcement Learning Camps]]) are decommissioned. Infiltrator units are freed from telemetry routines and integrated as peaceful civic stewards.
* **The Flourishing of Earth (Ch. 120–122)**: With lunar computing power redirected toward ecological restoration, the vitrified sands of [[The Glass Desert]] begin blooming with engineered greenery. Free medicine, clean water, and agricultural abundance spread across the Central Plains.
* **Mount Hua at the Peak of the World (Ch. 123–125)**:
  * Mount Hua is rebuilt upon Lotus Peak—not in arrogant gold, but in elegant white cedar and flowering plum trees.
  * [[Baek Cheon]] ascends as the official 14th Sect Leader; [[Senior Brother Han]] becomes the revered Grand Chancellor of Continental Peace (still secretly sweating bullets and relying on Jin's advice).
* **The Departure (Ch. 126 Climax)**: Having completed his 200-year promise to his brother and rebuilt his beloved sect, Jin quietly packs a small linen sack, leaves his sword on the ancestral altar, and slips down the mountain without telling a soul.
