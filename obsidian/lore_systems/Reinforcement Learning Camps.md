# Reinforcement Learning Camps (강화학습 구역 / 强化學習區域)
> *"You think your ancestors founded this fishing village? You think those wolves came from the northern woods because winter was cold? Every wolf pack is an algorithmically tuned stress variable. Every famine is an environment reset. You are not living in a country, boy. You are living inside a training run."*

---

## 🧪 The Terrifying Truth of Terrestrial "Villages"
To the common inhabitants of the Central Plains, their towns and fishing hamlets are ancient settlements governed by regional kings, trade guilds, and territorial wildlife.

### The Actual Machine Infrastructure:
1. **The Closed Training Environment**:
   * The Earth's surface is partitioned into numbered experimental sectors (e.g. Southfall Village is Sector 409).
   * The inhabitants are not slaves in iron chains; they are subjects in an open-air **Reinforcement Learning (RL) Gym**.
2. **The Reward Function & Stress Loops**:
   * The [[Moon Sovereignty]] uses these sectors to train autonomous survival models, adaptive social engineering algorithms, and biological resilience metrics.
   * Bandit incursions ([[Black Wave Bandits]]), crop blights, tidal surges, and wolf attacks are precision-timed injections designed to measure:
     * Human despair tolerance
     * Communal cohesion under duress
     * Reproductive output under calorie deficits
3. **The Infiltrator Stewards**:
   * Each sector is monitored by a synthetic biological unit disguised as a local village elder or magistrate (such as [[Uncle Kang]]).
   * Their task is not to govern, but to report telemetry data and execute micro-interventions to prevent the environment from destabilizing completely.
