# Qi as Non-Computability (기 / 氣)
> *"A rock falls at nine point eight meters per second squared. A bullet travels along an parabolic arc determined by gunpowder grain, air density, and rifling twist. A machine can predict a bullet before the hammer falls. But when a true swordsman draws his blade with his life on the line... mathematics is blind."*

---

## 🔬 Scientific & Ontological Definition
In the universe of *The Broken Sky & The Plum Blossom*, Qi is **not** magical fairy dust or vague spiritual energy. It is **biological non-computable emergence**.

### The Algorithmic Architecture:
1. **The Deterministic World**: The [[Moon Sovereignty]]’s neural networks calculate Newtonian mechanics, thermodynamic entropy, human biological baselines, and kinetic ballistics with $99.999\%$ predictive accuracy. To the machine, all ordinary human behavior is an autoregressive next-token prediction.
2. **The Emergent Breach (Qi)**:
   * When a human practitioner unites breath, physiological adrenaline, and irrational moral will (e.g. sacrificial resolve, pure martial pride), their consciousness transitions into a non-algorithmic state.
   * As proven by Turing and Penrose, true consciousness and creative intent cannot be compressed into a finite algorithmic simulation.
   * **Result**: An AI model cannot forecast the trajectory of a Qi-infused strike until *after* the strike has occurred. The sword creates a localized tear in causal determinism.

## ⚖️ Costs & Strict Biological Limitations
To maintain high narrative tension (Sanderson's Second Law: *Limitations are greater than powers*):
* **Metabolic Burn**: Cycling Qi violently oxidizes biological glucose, ATP, and blood glycogen. A martial artist who fights without sufficient calorie replenishment will suffer organ failure, hypoglycemic shock, and severe muscle atrophy.
* **Vessel Strain**: Forcing massive, non-computable currents of Qi through unrefined biological meridians tears fascia, ruptures blood vessels, and fractures bones. This is why [[Chung Jin]]'s 15-year-old malnourished body in [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]] and [[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]] constantly threatens to break under his ancient techniques.
* **The Thermal Signature Hazard**: Massive Qi output radiates biological infrared anomalies that trigger satellite heat sensors in orbit, provoking immediate retaliatory strikes from [[The Death Note Sky Laser]].
