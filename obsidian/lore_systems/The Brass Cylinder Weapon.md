## ☢️ The Singularity Munition
The brass cylinder was the peak superweapon engineered by pre-war technocrats during the collapse of the old order.
* **The Weapon Mechanism**: When the twin counter-rotating brass rings are locked, an internal magnetic constriction field collapses heavy isotopes into a micro-singularity, releasing an omnidirectional pulse of hard gamma rays, heat, and EMP.
* **The Aftermath**: Incinerated three hundred thousand soldiers in an instant, vitrifying the soil into iridescent green obsidian and scorching the atmosphere.
* **The Myth**: In the modern era, the weapon is remembered in folk songs as the "Wrath of the Fire God" or "Heaven's White Flash."
