> *"There is no thunder. There is no pillar of fire falling from the sky. A scholar sits at his desk, dips his brush in ink, and solves the three-body problem. He blinks once, grasps his temple, and drops dead. The coroner calls it an apoplexy. The Moon calls it garbage collection."*

## 🎯 The Ultimate Instrument of Cognitive Pruning
The primary danger of the [[Moon Sovereignty]] is not massive nuclear bombardment—which would destroy the precious biological dataset—but **invisible, targeted assassination**.

### Technical Mechanics:
* **The Microwave Frequency**: The lunar satellites focus an ultra-narrow coherent beam of high-frequency microwave radiation through atmospheric refraction windows.
* **The Clinical Result**: The beam boils a cubic millimeter of cerebrospinal fluid inside the target's Circle of Willis in under 40 milliseconds. The victim suffers an instantaneous, fatal rupture—appearing to any primitive doctor as a natural stroke or brain aneurysm.
* **The Trigger Thresholds**:
  * Discovering calculus, quantum mechanics, or radio transmission.
  * Attempting to construct vacuum tubes or electrical dynamo coils.
  * Reaching the transcendent 8th tier of non-computable sword cultivation ([[Qi as Non-Computability]]).
* **Why Jin Must Move in Shadows**: Throughout the early arcs, [[Chung Jin]] cannot simply reveal his cosmic sword skills or overthrow regional courts with blinding displays of power. Doing so would trip lunar thermal and non-computable sensors, prompting an immediate stroke from the sky.
