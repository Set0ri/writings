> *"Duty does not pause while you weep for your cousins, Jin. When cancer invades the blood, the surgeon who pities the limb kills the body. Draw the seal. Fire the weapon!"*

## ⚖️ The Ancient Clash of Ideologies
Two centuries before Jin’s awakening in Southfall, the climax of human history occurred upon the plains of Kurukshetra.
* **The Extremist Heresy**: An extremist crusade arose preaching that humanity was an incurable disease and that all artificial intelligence had to be purged from the galaxy to reset the cosmic slate.
* **The Seduction of Kin**: Jin's own relatives, martial peers, and childhood friends were radicalized by this nihilistic doctrine.
* **The Dharma Dilemma**: Drawing direct inspiration from the *Bhagavad Gita*, Jin was commanded to discharge [[The Brass Cylinder Weapon]] to eliminate the fanatic army. Jin froze upon seeing the faces of those he loved.
* **The Cataclysm**: Jin's hesitation led to the death of [[Elder Brother]] and forced Jin to detonate the munition in pure agony, turning the plains into vitrified green glass ([[The Glass Desert]]).
