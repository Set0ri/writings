# The Trishula (त्रिशूल)

> *"He swung the Trishula upward with the dying ember of his soul. In that flash of crackling bronze and primordial lightning, the arm of the Heavenly Demon King fell like a severed mountain."*

## 1. Overview & Cosmology
The **Trishula** is the legendary three-pronged divine relic-trident associated with the archetype of Lord Shiva and preserved within the deepest vaults of [[Mount Hua Plum Blossom Sect]] at [[Mount Kailash]]. Unlike the standard forged steel of mortal martial artists, the Trishula is an ancient pre-collapse relic composed of resonating hyper-dense bronze alloy interlaced with superconducting Qi channels.

## 2. Functions & Sacred Geometry
* **The Three Tines (Tri-Guna / Tri-Shakti)**:
  1. *Creation (Srishti)*: Channeling vital life-force and unbridled biological Qi.
  2. *Preservation (Sthiti)*: Maintaining spatial coherence and kinetic equilibrium.
  3. *Destruction (Samhara)*: Disrupting electromagnetic and localized gravitational fields.
* **The Severing of the Demon King**: In [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]], while mortally impaled on the black blade of [[The Heavenly Demon]], [[Elder Brother]] drew the Trishula to cleanly sever the Demon King's right arm at the shoulder, ending his absolute kinetic superiority.
* **The Catalyst of Ascension**: Following Kamina's death, [[Chung Jin]] claimed the Trishula, channeling his grief and dharmanic resolve through its three prongs to hover into the sky, chanting *"Om Agni, Om Shiva"* as he unlocked [[The Solar Lotus & The Two Keys]].

## 3. Narrative Significance & The Memory Gap
In the present day, following Jin's reincarnation in [[Southfall Village]], the conscious memory of wielding the Trishula is fragmented. Jin recalls hovering in the black sky holding a crackling trident, but the exact whereabouts of the Trishula after the whiteout remain one of the paramount cosmic mysteries of Book 1.
