# Radiant Oaths of Truth (진실의 서약 / 眞實誓約)
> *"You cannot advance by simply circulating Qi through your meridians ten thousand times like a waterwheel. A bottleneck in cultivation is never a physical blockage; it is a lie you tell yourself to avoid weeping in the dark."*

---

## 🧠 The Psychological Architecture of Progression
In traditional cultivation novels, power scaling is achieved by eating rare herbs or finding ancient pills. Here, following Brandon Sanderson's design principles, **breakthroughs are gated by psychological truth**.

### The 4 Tiers of Internal Revelation:
1. **First Tier (The Mortal Mirror)**: Recognizing one’s physical frailty and discarding false pride. (Achieved by [[Baek Cheon]] when he abandons his aristocratic vanity to wield Jin’s wooden stick).
2. **Second Tier (The Vow of Responsibility)**: Accepting that pain cannot be projected onto innocent others. (Achieved by [[Cousin Bao]] when he chooses to protect his aunt rather than strike down weaker children).
3. **Third Tier (The Severance of the Ideal)**: Acknowledging that righteous intentions do not prevent catastrophe. (Achieved by [[Senior Brother Han]] when he steps forward to face real death with zero cultivation).
4. **Fourth Tier (The Forgiveness of the Self)**: Confronting the core traumatic memory without denial. (Achieved by [[Chung Jin]] when he admits aloud that his hesitation doomed his brother, releasing two centuries of subconscious terror).
