# Yeon (연 / 蓮)
> *"Eat slowly, Jin. When the wind is screaming off the sea and the magistrates are shouting in the streets, a hot bowl of rice with pickled turnip is the only true peace in this world. Don't look at the sky with such hard eyes. A home is built with hands, not blades."*

---

## 🍵 The Hearth that Dissolves 200 Years of Trauma
Yeon is gentle, perceptive, and hardworking. While Jin was an ancient warlord with the blood of millions on his hands from Kurukshetra, Yeon’s unconditional maternal warmth disarms his cynicism and grounds him in physical human reality.

### Narrative Significance:
* **The Antidote to Cosmic Cynicism**: When Jin awakens complaining about his scrawny frame and aching bones, Yeon places warm herbal compresses on his forehead and wraps his feet in patched quilts.
* **The Root of [[The Anti-Adventure Warning]]**: In [[Arc 14 - Return to the Soil]], the philosophy Jin imparts to future generations stems directly from Yeon’s domestic wisdom: that conquest, godhood, and boundless martial ascension are barren compared to tending the soil and sharing bread with those you love.
