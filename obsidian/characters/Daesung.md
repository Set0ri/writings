# Daesung (대성 / 大成)
> *"My mother held my hand on the straw mat before she closed her eyes forever, Jin. She made me swear by the river ancestors: 'Protect your younger brother Kang. No matter what he does, never let him starve.' A vow isn't something you put down when your shoulders hurt. A man carries it until the dirt covers his eyes."*

---

## 🌲 The Honest Hunter of Southfall
Daesung is a tall, broad-shouldered hunter whose knuckles are scarred by boar spears and winter frost. He represents the silent, enduring dignity of common humanity under colonial oppression.

### Key Dynamics:
* **The Filial Curse**: Despite Uncle Kang's ruthless extortion and open contempt, Daesung never raised a fist against his younger brother. He endured humiliation and double taxes because of an unbreakable deathbed promise made to his dying mother.
* **The Father's Protection**: When Uncle Kang ordered wolves and bandits to surround their home in [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]], Daesung stood with an iron hunting fork at the broken gate, ready to bleed out to buy five seconds of life for his wife and son.
* **Liberation**: Jin’s ruthless intervention frees Daesung from the spiritual prison of the vow by exposing that the real Kang had long been dead and replaced by an algorithmic copy.
