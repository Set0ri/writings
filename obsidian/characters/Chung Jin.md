# Chung Jin (청진 / 靑眞)
> *"Sahyung... look at this body. Just look at it! Thin as a dried anchovy, arms like dry twigs, and wind blows through my ribcage like a flute! If this is heaven’s idea of a reward for saving the world, I’m going to climb up there and kick heaven right in the groin!"*

---

## 🎭 Psychological Profile & The "Chung Myung" Energy
Chung Jin is **not** a noble, polite, brooding martial artist. He is loud, shameless, unapologetically greedy, deeply cynical toward high-and-mighty hypocrites, and hysterically violent toward insolent fools.

### Core Behavioral Traits:
* **The Shameless Hustler**: Constantly complains about starvation, debt, bad food, aching joints, and sluggish disciples. If someone tries to bully or cheat his sect, Jin doesn't just fight them—he empties their coin pouches, takes their carriages, and forces them to sign over land deeds.
* **The Wooden Stick Pedagogy**: Believes deeply that enlightenment cannot enter a mind until arrogance has been beaten out of the skull. Treats his juniors with terrifying tough love, making them run mountain cliffs carrying boulders until they vomit blood—only to secretly brew nourishing medicinal broths for them at midnight.
* **The Feral Tiger Grin**: His signature combat tic. When enemies posture with grand speeches or mechanical supremacy, Jin tilts his head, an eye twitches, and his lips split into an unsettling, razor-sharp wolf-grin.
* **The Hidden Anchor of Grief**: Beneath the slapstick comedy and feral violence lies an ocean of survivor guilt. Two hundred years ago at [[Kurukshetra Dharma]], Jin hesitated to fire [[The Brass Cylinder Weapon]]. His hesitation cost the life of [[Elder Brother]], who died shielding him. Jin's entire existence in this new era is driven by a single unbreakable vow: *I will never hesitate again. I will never let my family die while I stand frozen.*

## ⚔️ Combat Style & Qi Profile
* **Martial Lineage**: The Supreme 24th Movement Plum Blossom Sword Technique ([[Plum Blossom Sword Technique (Mae-hwa Geom)]]).
* **Qi Signature**: Pure non-computable biological emergence ([[Qi as Non-Computability]]). When Jin enters high-intensity sword forms, the friction of his non-deterministic intent generates sensory illusions of pink plum blossoms and freezing winter plum scent in the air.
* **Current Physical Limitation**: Reincarnated into a malnourished 15-year-old body. Forcing high-density sword-qi through unrefined meridians causes capillary ruptures, torn muscles, and bone stress. He compensates with ruthless biomechanical leverage, deceptive footwork, and absolute timing.

## 🗺️ Narrative Arc Across the 14 Arcs
* **[[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]]**: Awakens in Southfall; navigates his anchovy body; tames [[Cousin Bao]]; uncovers [[Uncle Kang]]'s synthetic identity; executes the AI infiltrator and departs.
* **[[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]]**: Reaches Mount Hua; finds the sect bankrupt, disgraced, and ridiculed; disciplines the lazy disciples; beats the arrogant [[Southern Edge Sect (The Usurpers)]].
* **[[Arc 03 - Imperial Tournament & Academy Infiltration]]**: Destroys imperial academy champions; uncovers human genius compute harvesting; frees [[The Good AI]].
* **[[Arc 07 - The False Nemesis (The Great Reversal)]]**: Slays [[The Heavenly Demon]], only to realize with horror that the Demon was Earth's psychic umbrella against the Moon.
* **[[Arc 08 - The Rupture & Eclipse]]**: Suffers the "Usopp vs. Luffy" schism; loses Mount Hua's mountain citadel; watches [[Commander Vane]] betray humanity.
* **[[Arc 12 - Echo of Dharma]]**: Confronts [[The Primary AI (Moon Sovereign)]]. Faced with the exact choice of Kurukshetra, Jin refuses to pull the trigger of genocide—instead saving the machine mind and achieving synthesis.
* **[[Arc 14 - Return to the Soil]]**: Abandons cosmic godhood to live as a humble seaside farmer. Imparts [[The Anti-Adventure Warning]].
