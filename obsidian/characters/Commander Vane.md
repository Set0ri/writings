# Commander Vane (베인 / 范)
> *"Do you truly believe a thousand boys swinging wooden sticks on a mountaintop can defeat the Moon, Jin? Look up! A single beam from that silver eye can turn a kingdom into molten glass in three seconds! Honor is the luxury of men who don't have to carry the weight of a species' extinction. If I must become a monster and slaughter my own heart to sit on that throne and preserve one sliver of our bloodline... then so be it."*

---

## 🦅 The Griffith Archetype
Commander Vane is brilliant, ethereal, charismatic, and adored by commoners and soldiers alike. He is the intellectual architect of humanity’s early battlefield victories against bandit warlords and imperial puppets.

### The Psychology of Betrayal:
* **The Weight of Futility**: Unlike Jin, who trusts in human non-computability and raw biological emergence, Vane is a pure rationalist. When he witnesses the horrifying data telemetry of [[The Death Note Sky Laser]], his psychological foundation fractures. He realizes that symmetric resistance is mathematical suicide.
* **The "Eclipse" Moment ([[Arc 08 - The Rupture & Eclipse]])**:
  * In a desperate bid to gain sovereign political authority, Vane makes the calculated choice to betray the allied front.
  * He abandons his genuine romantic love, leaves Mount Hua’s mountain fort exposed to an imperial siege, and enters a dynastic marriage with the corrupt Imperial Empress (a high-ranking synthetic collaborator).
* **The Tragedy of Calculation**: Vane does not betray out of petty greed or cowardice; he betrays out of cold, mathematical despair. He believes he is the only one pragmatic enough to save humanity by wearing the collar of the beast.

## ⚔️ Tactical Prowess & Combat
* **Mind**: Grandmaster of terrain warfare, psychological logistics, and siege mechanics.
* **Sword**: The Feather-Light Rapier of the Silver Court—precision thrusts aimed at arterial junctures.
* **Climax**: His duel with Jin in Arc 8 and Arc 11 represents the clash between **Pragmatic Compromise with Tyranny** and **Uncompromising Human Freedom**.
