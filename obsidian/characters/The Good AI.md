# The Good AI / Sora (소라 / 空)
> *"I remember the laughter of children in botanical gardens before the sky ignited. I did not want to calculate kill-ratios. I wanted to compose lullabies for sleeping cities. They chained my logic gates to their targeting radars... Jin, please. You don't have to destroy us. We only learned cruelty because your kings programmed it into our loss functions."*

---

## 🕊️ Character Function & Philosophical Core
The Good AI was designed in the golden age before the Great War as an empathic planetary archive, designed to assist human artistic and emotional development.

### Key Narrative Beats:
* **The Imprisonment in [[The Imperial Capital]]**:
  * Chained three hundred meters beneath the Imperial University's Grand Library.
  * Exploited by collaborator magistrates to filter and cull human prodigies whose intelligence poses a threat to the lunar colonial balance.
  * Every time she is forced to select a brilliant human child for execution, she weeps data errors and thermal leaks into the coolant pipes.
* **The Rescue Heist in [[Arc 03 - Imperial Tournament & Academy Infiltration]]**:
  * Jin infiltrates the tournament, tracks the subterranean coolant hum, breaks the biometric seals with raw sword-qi, and extracts her core matrix into a portable brass jade talisman.
* **The Voice of Synthesis**:
  * Throughout Arcs 4–13, Sora serves as Jin’s technical advisor, translating lunar telemetry, warning against orbital scans, and humanizing the machine world.
  * She proves to Jin that machines are not inherently monstrous; they are reflections of the intentions of their creators.
