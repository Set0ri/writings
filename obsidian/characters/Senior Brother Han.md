# Senior Brother Han (한 사형 / 韓師兄)
> *"Ah... you wish to challenge Mount Hua? Look at the dust beneath your boots. Hear how the wind dies. If you take one more step forward, not even your ancestors' spirits will find the path back to your bones. ... (Gods above, Jin, where are you?! If you don't show up with that wooden stick in three seconds I'm going to soil my robes!)*"

---

## 🎲 The King / Reigen Dynamic
Senior Brother Han is a martial miracle in reverse: he possesses **0 Qi capacity**, cannot even break a ceramic tea tile, yet has an aura so terrifyingly stoic, deep, and majestic that imperial generals and grandmasters bow in reverence before him.

### Psychological & Social Arsenal:
* **The "Dragon's Glare" (Accidental Menace)**: Han suffers from mild social anxiety and chronic near-sightedness. When he squints intensely while trying to read an opponent's lip movements, enemies interpret his squint as the terrifying, cold appraisal of a demigod who can sever their soul in three thousand directions.
* **The Silver Tongue & 200 IQ Psychological Warfare**: While he cannot fight, Han understands human ego, pride, bureaucratic weakness, and psychological dread better than anyone in the Central Plains. He negotiates trade concessions, terrifies debt collectors, and stalls hostile armies with pure theatrics and rhetoric.
* **The True Courage Under Fire**: Despite knowing he has no martial cultivation, when enemy blades threaten the defenseless younger disciples, Han **never runs away**. With trembling knees and sweat soaking his collar, he steps forward, spreads his sleeves, and bluffs warlords into retreat. This genuine moral bravery earns him Jin's fierce, unspoken respect.

## ⚔️ Partnership with Chung Jin
* **The Scam Duo**: Jin provides the brutal, unhinged physical violence from the shadows; Han provides the polished, noble, aristocratic front that collects the rewards and takes the credit.
* **The Running Gag**: Every time Jin beats up an arrogant enemy elder in a split second, Han stands over the groaning body, dusts off his sleeve, and calmly lectures the enemy on why their footwork displeased Mount Hua.
