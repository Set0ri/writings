# Baek Cheon (백천 / 白天)
> *"I am Baek Cheon of Mount Hua! We do not grovel in the mud for copper coins! We do not ambush merchants in alleys! We are a righteous sect of... Jin, put down that club! Put it down! Fine, fine! I'll dig the irrigation ditch! Just don't aim for the back of my head again!"*

---

## 🌸 The Noble Pride Shattered & Reborn
Baek Cheon was born into high aristocracy but joined Mount Hua out of idealistic romanticism, only to watch the sect crumble into debt, mockery, and ruin.

### Character Evolution:
* **The Pompous Aristocrat**: When Jin first arrives at Mount Hua, Baek Cheon clings desperately to empty rituals, elaborate silk robes with frayed edges, and flowery, useless sword forms that have lost their lethal edge.
* **The Wooden Stick Baptism**: Jin systematically breaks Baek Cheon’s pride with a heavy wooden cudgel, proving that flowery forms without weight or intent are worthless against real blood and iron.
* **The True Pillar of Mount Hua**: As Jin drives the disciples through agonizing physical regimens, Baek Cheon sheds his vanity. He becomes Mount Hua’s steadfast vanguard leader—combining his natural grace with Jin's ruthless, unyielding tenacity.
