## 🏛️ The Conscience of Mount Hua
Yoon Jong is the earnest, disciplined mediator who tries to uphold sect rules, morality, and historical decorum.

### Dynamic:
* **The Sisyphus of Mount Hua**: Spends eighty percent of his waking hours trying to stop Jin from doing something illegal, blasphemous, or extortionate—and the remaining twenty percent apologizing to bewildered guests.
* **The Disciples' Anchor**: When Jin pushes the juniors to their physical and psychological breaking point, Yoon Jong is the brother who binds their sprains, splits his bread with them, and reminds them what they are enduring it all for.
