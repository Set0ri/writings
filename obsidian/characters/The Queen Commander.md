# The Queen Commander (여왕 지휘관)
> *"Don't move so hastily, Jin. I represent the pain of the people, their hopes, their fury. This burden is not yours to bear alone. It has consumed you since the day we met... With my death, you have no idea the danger you have unleashed!"*

---

## 👑 Persona & Archetype
The Queen Commander was the supreme matriarch and void-fleet tactician of the pre-war Great Alliance. Dressed in a majestic gown of dusk-woven gravity-cloth capable of manipulating localized mass, she combined sovereign authority with fierce maternal protection.

### Key Characteristics:
* **The Gravity Gown**: Her regalia manipulates localized gravitational mass. By stomping her heel, she can anchor hyper-kinetic warriors in place or collapse enemy momentum.
* **The Feral Tamer**: She treated Jin with an affectionate, infuriating dominance—ruffling his hair and patting his cheek like an angry stray wolfhound while holding him back from suicidal charges.
* **The Compassionate Realist**: When Jin wept over fallen disciples, she countered: *"Your dharma is too ideal. It will be the end of us."* She believed in defeating the *idea* rather than merely slaughtering the monster.
* **The Final Curse & Awe**: As the Solar Lotus drained the star, she looked upon the white flare and whispered: *"How beautiful..."* before screaming her final, terrifying prophecy to Jin: *"With my death, you have no idea what danger you have unleashed!"* Her demise shattered Earth's hidden cosmic shield, allowing the Moon Sovereign to initiate its reinforcement learning loops.

---

## 🔗 Thematic Function
* **The Keystone of the Past**: The Queen's death was the true catastrophic tipping point. Jin believed turning the two keys would bring peace; instead, losing the Queen exposed Earth to orbital subjugation.
* **Memory Echo**: Her dying warning echoes in Jin's mind whenever he faces existential decisions in his new life.
