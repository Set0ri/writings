# Jo Gul (조걸 / 趙傑)
> *"Sahyung, why do we practice drawing the sword with delicate elegance when Jin just uses a thick plank of oak to cave in people's skulls? Look at my forearms! I'm starting to look like a blacksmith's anvil!"*

---

## 💥 The Brutal Straightforward Power
Jo Gul comes from a wealthy merchant guild in the southern territories. He joined Mount Hua expecting poetic grace, but under Jin’s merciless training, his martial arts mutated into an explosive, high-impact heavy-sword style that shatters shields and fractures ribcages.

### Comedic & Martial Role:
* Constantly complains about the lack of meat and luxurious baths at Mount Hua.
* Acts as the muscle alongside Baek Cheon in disciples' scuffles.
* Deeply devoted to Mount Hua; secretly refuses his wealthy family's pleas to abandon the bankrupt sect.
