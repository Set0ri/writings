# Dark Kaladin / Mu-Gwang (무광 / 霧光)
> *"I only wanted a bowl of millet for my little sister. Just one bowl. They took her teeth for porcelain dentures and threw her into the lime pits. And you tell me humanity is worth preserving? Look at what we do to each other! The machine is right. We are a disease that must be burned down to the roots."*

---

## 🌑 The Tragic Mirror to Chung Jin
If Chung Jin represents the determination to laugh, hustle, and rebuild in the face of absolute loss, **Dark Kaladin** is the living embodiment of what happens when unbearable grief meets unyielding hatred.

### Psychological Degeneration:
* **The Pure Beginnings**: Starts as an impoverished boy from the mining sectors wielding an old spear, fiercely protective of his starving siblings. He is honest, selfless, and self-sacrificing.
* **The Systematic Crushing**: At every turn, the corrupt human collaborator nobility ([[Imperial Academies & Central Dynasty]]) betrays, exploits, and murders those he loves. His sister dies of preventable sepsis while imperial magistrates feed meat dumplings to ornamental dogs.
* **Adoption of the Pre-War Heresy**: Broken by suffering, he discovers ancient pre-war scriptures preaching the extremist creed that triggered the Kurukshetra war: *"Human nature is flawed beyond redemption; only complete annihilation can cleanse the stars."*
* **The Foil Dynamic with Jin**:
  * Jin lost his world $\rightarrow$ responds with feral humor, aggressive love, and pragmatic rebuilding.
  * Kaladin lost his world $\rightarrow$ responds with ascetic martyrdom, righteous fury, and cosmic genocide.
  * In combat, Jin’s plum blossoms bloom with fragrant spring life; Kaladin’s spear drains all color and warmth from the air, leaving only cold gray ash.

## ⚔️ Combat & Spear Art
* **Weapon**: A jagged black spear crafted from rusted pre-war rebar.
* **Cultivation**: Ashen Void Qi—a parasitic cultivation technique that converts the user's despair and the opponent's vitality into devastating kinetic thrusts.
* **Climax in [[Arc 06 - Shadow of the Heavenly Demon]] & [[Arc 11 - The Lunar Core & Sky Laser]]**: Serves as the ultimate tragic barrier Jin must dismantle not with superior violence, but with philosophical truth.
