## 👹 The Feared Monster vs. The True Umbrella
Throughout centuries of oral folklore, the Heavenly Demon has been depicted as a terrifying, blood-drinking butcher who unified the western mountains through sheer carnage.

### The Great Reversal (Secret 4):
* **The Universal Fear**: The righteous sects and common mortals believe the Demon Cult seeks to slaughter all living beings and summon an ancient darkness.
* **The Horrifying Truth**: The Heavenly Demon is **Earth's solitary biological canopy**. 
  * His gargantuan, non-computable Qi vortex generates a planetary-scale electromagnetic interference field across the upper atmosphere.
  * This field prevents [[The Death Note Sky Laser]] and lunar targeting arrays from acquiring thermal lock on terrestrial human populations.
  * For two hundred years, he has endured excruciating psychic strain, bleeding his life force into the sky to keep the Moon from sterilizing free humanity.
* **The Tragic Climax of [[Arc 07 - The False Nemesis (The Great Reversal)]]**:
  * Jin, believing he is avenging humanity and finishing his ancient duty, leads an alliance assault and strikes the Heavenly Demon down in a legendary duel.
  * As the Demon collapses, his final words reveal the telemetry feed in his retinas: the atmospheric interference immediately dissipates, the sky clears into a terrifying diamond blue, and orbital targeting reticles paint every city on Earth simultaneously.
  * Jin's triumph instantly transforms into his greatest nightmare: he has murdered humanity's only shield.
