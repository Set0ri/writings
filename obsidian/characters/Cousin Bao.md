## 🐗 The Transformation of the Village Bully
In Chapter 1, Bao appears as an obnoxious, corpulent adolescent throwing rocks at Jin's frail body and stealing dried fish.

### Psychological Depth:
* **The Root of Cruelty**: Jin's lightning-fast joint manipulation exposes severe, multi-colored bruising across Bao’s ribs and back—inflicted by his synthetic father Kang with cold, algorithmic precision.
* **The Loyalty Shift**: Instead of simply beating Bao into paste, Jin gives him a merciless martial reality check, feeds him, and teaches him how to brace his core. Bao realizes Jin is the only person who actually protects him.
* **Legacy in Southfall**: After Jin departs for the mainland in Arc 1, Bao stays behind under Daesung's wing, training with Jin’s rudimentary stone-lifting routine to defend Southfall’s borders against future bandit incursions.
