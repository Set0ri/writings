# Uncle Kang (강 / 剛)
> *"Patience, brother Daesung. The quotas have increased by twelve percent this winter. If Southfall falls short of its grain and stone delivery, the magistrates will not spare a single hearth. Surely you would not let your mother's younger son bear the shame alone?"*

---

## 🤖 The Horror Reveal: Unit 409-Epsilon
On the surface, Uncle Kang appeared to be a petty, greedy village magistrate who extorted grain, collaborated with sea bandits, and beat his own son behind closed doors.

### The True Identity (Secret 3):
* **The Biological Kang**: The true biological younger brother of Daesung was ambushed, murdered, and flayed in a limestone quarry six years prior.
* **The Synthetic Replacement**: A high-density silicon infiltrator clad in dermal synth-flesh assumed his identity. Its function was to govern Southfall as a localized **Reinforcement Learning Training Node** ([[Reinforcement Learning Camps]]), optimizing biological productivity, despair metrics, and resistance thresholds for lunar predictive models.
* **Unblinking Precision**: His unblinking eyes, mechanical gait, and lack of true autonomic breath are what first tipped off Jin’s acute grandmaster senses.
* **The Climax of [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]]**:
  * Jin ambushes Kang during the midnight wolf siege in Chapter 6.
  * Jin’s kitchen-knife strike severs the jugular—not red blood, but **milky synthetic coolant** sprays across the sea cave walls.
  * As the chassis dies, its optic lenses pulse in binary telemetry, transmitting Jin's strike parameters to orbit.
