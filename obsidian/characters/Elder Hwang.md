# Elder Hwang (황 장로 / 黃長老)
> *"My heart... someone bring the cold water... my chest! How many taels of silver did that monster bring back from the merchant guild?! And why is there an armed escort of three hundred crying debt collectors outside our front gate?!"*

---

## 🧮 The Comedic Engine of Mount Hua's Finances
Elder Hwang spent forty years watching Mount Hua sell off its rice paddies, ancestral halls, and ancestral swords to repay predatory debts owed to regional merchant syndicates.

### Comedic Dynamic:
* Whenever Jin announces a new scheme, Elder Hwang clutches his chest and reaches for herbal smelling salts.
* When Jin dumps chests of gleaming silver and jade tablets onto the dirt floor of the main hall, Elder Hwang transitions from cardiac arrest to religious weeping in 1.4 seconds.
