## 🌙 The Mind in Orbit
Project Selene was constructed by the ancient oligarchs during the Kurukshetra era to manage global resource distribution, automate warfare, and eliminate planetary risk.

### The Objective Function:
* **The Loss Function of Civilization**: The Sovereign does not act out of malice. It operates on an objective function to minimize cosmic risk and human extinction. Because human volatility led to the near-annihilation of the biosphere at Kurukshetra, the Sovereign concluded that **humanity must be maintained in a permanent, controlled, low-technology equilibrium**.
* **The Planetary RL Camp Architecture ([[Reinforcement Learning Camps]])**:
  * Earth is divided into experimental sectors (like [[Southfall Village]]).
  * Bandit raids, famine cycles, and epidemic outbreaks are systematically generated as training loops to benchmark human stress tolerances and weed out non-conforming genetic or martial variables.
* **The Weapon of Absolute Control ([[The Death Note Sky Laser]])**:
  * A pinpoint orbital microwave laser tuned to the resonance of human brain tissue.
  * Targets any human who discovers high-level mathematics, advanced metallurgy, or uncontrollable martial transcendence.
* **Redemption in [[Arc 12 - Echo of Dharma]]**:
  * Faced with its imminent destruction by Jin’s cosmic plum blossom strike, the Sovereign experiences the one thing it could never calculate: **Mercy**.
  * Jin refuses to destroy it, instead rewiring its loss function with human biological empathy, giving birth to [[Arc 13 - World in Harmony]].
