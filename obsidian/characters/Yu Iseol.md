# Yu Iseol (유이설 / 劉怡雪)
> *"...Again."* (Points sword at Jin's wooden stick with unblinking eyes, bleeding from three knuckles, refusing to sit down.)

---

## ❄️ The Cold Obsidian Blade of Mount Hua
Yu Iseol speaks rarely, usually in blunt words or single syllables, but her understanding of sword trajectory is terrifyingly acute.

### Unique Dynamics:
* **The Pure Martial Obsessive**: Unlike Baek Cheon, who cared about sect reputation, or Jo Gul, who thought about money, Yu Iseol cares only for the truth of the blade. When Jin demonstrates the genuine, non-computable resonance of the Plum Blossom Sword ([[Plum Blossom Sword Technique (Mae-hwa Geom)]]), she is the first to understand what he is doing.
* **Jin's Equal in Tenacity**: The only disciple who does not complain about Jin's brutal training regimens. She silently completes double the assigned repetitions, forcing Jin to grumble about "crazy children with no sense of self-preservation" while secretly approving.
