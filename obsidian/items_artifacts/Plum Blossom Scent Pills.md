# Plum Blossom Scent Pills (매화단 / 梅花丹)
The proprietary medicinal pills secretly brewed by Jin in the dead of night using wild mountain plums, honey, and crushed roots. While he screams at his disciples by day and hits them with wooden sticks, he leaves these fragrant pink pills on their windowsills so their broken muscles recover overnight.
