# Wooden Practice Sword (목검 / 木劍)
> *"A good blade cuts the flesh. A solid oak stick beats humility directly into the marrow. Which one do you think you need more today, Baek Cheon?"*

Jin's signature weapon of comedic terror throughout [[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]] and [[Arc 03 - Imperial Tournament & Academy Infiltration]]. With it, Jin shatters formal sword stances, disarms imperial prodigies, and traumatizes debt collectors.
