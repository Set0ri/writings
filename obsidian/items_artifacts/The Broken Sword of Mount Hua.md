# The Broken Sword of Mount Hua (화산의 부러진 검 / 華山殘劍)
The ancient forged steel blade carried by Jin during the Great War. Snapped in two when Jin struck down his fanatic kin after [[Elder Brother]]'s death. Preserved in the dusty ruins of Mount Hua as a reminder of the sect's sacrifice.
