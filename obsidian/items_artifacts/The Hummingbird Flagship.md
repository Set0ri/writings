# The Hummingbird Flagship

> *"Floating beside the cratered regolith of the Moon was the Hummingbird—a colossal, brutalist capital ship carved in the silhouette of a predatory avian ornithopter. Its multi-jointed kinetic wings hummed with the power of a captured star."*

## 1. Overview & Aesthetics
The **Hummingbird** is the ultimate capital starship of the [[Mount Hua Plum Blossom Sect]]'s void vanguard. Designed with a stark, brutalist **Dune-style aesthetic**, the ship stretches over two leagues in length, forged from matte-graphite faceted hull plating with razor-thin angular lines. 

Unlike traditional boxy cruisers, the *Hummingbird* literally resembles an iridescent, predatory hummingbird:
* **Interlocking Mechanical Feather-Scales**: The hull is composed of thousands of overlapping titanium-graphite feather-scale plates that ripple and flex to dissipate atmospheric friction and laser fire.
* **Articulated Kinetic Wings**: Multi-segmented, swept-back wing baffles that vibrate at ultra-high harmonic frequencies, creating localized gravitational displacement and kinetic shielding.
* **Avian Beak Prow & Crown**: The forward command module is shaped like an elongated needle-beak, with an armored crown where crew can stand directly in the vacuum under localized kinetic containment fields.
* **Ventral Hangar Bays**: Massive cavernous drop bays on its underside, large enough to carry and launch heavy planetary assault craft like [[Elder Brother|The Giant Turtle Mech-Ship]].

## 2. Strategic Position at Kurukshetra & The Scale Reveal
During the events of [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall|the Kurukshetra catastrophe]], the *Hummingbird* was stationed in **cislunar orbit floating directly beside the Moon**, framed against the blinding flares of the distant sun. 

Seen from across the cosmos, it appeared deceptively delicate and small. Yet upon zooming in, its colossal scale was revealed: [[Elder Brother]] lay casually sleeping on his back atop the warm titanium plumage with his red coat fluttering into the abyss, while [[Chung Jin]] stood beside him holding a handheld brass spyglass to observe the vanguard at [[Mount Kailash]].

It was from the exterior hull of the *Hummingbird* that [[Chung Jin]] stepped off into the void, descending feet-first with arms crossed through the mesosphere to strike the earth.
