# The Hummingbird Flagship

> *"Floating beside the cratered regolith of the Moon was the Hummingbird—a colossal, brutalist capital ship carved in the silhouette of a predatory avian ornithopter. Its multi-jointed kinetic wings hummed with the power of a captured star."*

## 1. Overview & Aesthetics
The **Hummingbird** is the ultimate capital starship of the [[Mount Hua Plum Blossom Sect]]'s void vanguard. Designed with a stark, brutalist **Dune-style aesthetic**, the ship stretches over two leagues in length, forged from matte-graphite faceted hull plating with razor-thin angular lines. 

Unlike traditional boxy cruisers, the *Hummingbird* resembles a monolithic, predatory avian leviathan:
* **Articulated Kinetic Wings**: Multi-segmented, swept-back wing baffles that vibrate at ultra-high harmonic frequencies, creating localized gravitational displacement and kinetic shielding.
* **Avian Beak Prow**: The forward command module is shaped like an angular falcon's beak, equipped with amber panoramic sensor slits and quantum telemetry arrays.
* **Ventral Hangar Bays**: Massive cavernous drop bays on its underside, large enough to carry and launch heavy planetary assault craft like [[Elder Brother|The Giant Turtle Mech-Ship]].

## 2. Strategic Position at Kurukshetra
During the events of [[arcs/Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall|the Kurukshetra catastrophe]], the *Hummingbird* was stationed in **cislunar orbit floating directly beside the Moon**. From this commanding vantage point, [[Elder Brother]] and [[Chung Jin]] observed the invasion of the [[factions/Moon Sovereignty|Moon Sovereignty]] and the descent of [[The Heavenly Demon]]'s Flying Tech-Whale.

It was from the exterior hull of the *Hummingbird* that [[Chung Jin]] stepped off into the void, descending feet-first with arms crossed through the mesosphere to strike the earth at [[locations/Mount Kailash|Mount Kailash]].
