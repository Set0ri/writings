# Orbital Telemetry Relay (궤도 전송기 / 軌道傳送器)
Microscopic optical emitters embedded behind the retinas of synthetic overseers like [[Uncle Kang]], used to transmit battlefield biometrics, Qi frequency readings, and environment logs to [[The Primary AI (Moon Sovereign)]].
