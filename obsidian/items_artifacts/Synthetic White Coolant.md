# Synthetic White Coolant (합성 냉각유 / 人造白血)
The milky, odorless, non-conductive synthetic heat-transfer fluid circulating through AI skin-walker units like [[Uncle Kang]]. Its discovery by Jin in Arc 1 confirms that the world is under covert robotic supervision.
