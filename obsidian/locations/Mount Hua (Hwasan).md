# Mount Hua / Hwasan (화산 / 華山)
> *"Climbing the thousand stone steps of Lotus Peak in winter, every breath tastes like pine needles and freezing cloud. And at the summit, ten thousand plum blossoms defy the snow."*

The sacred mountain where [[Chung Jin]] returns in [[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]]. Though reduced to squalor for two hundred years, it is systematically restored into humanity's premier fortress of martial and philosophical resistance.
