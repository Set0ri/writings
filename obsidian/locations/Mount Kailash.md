# Mount Kailash & The Annapurna Ridge

> *"In the shadow of Mount Kailash, where the jagged ridges of Annapurna scraped against the upper heavens like teeth of unpolished jade... midday plummeted into pitch midnight."*

## 1. Geographical & Mythic Significance
**Mount Kailash** and the adjacent **Annapurna Ridge** form the geographic and mythic heart of the pre-collapse continent. Revered in ancient texts as the terrestrial abode of Shiva and the Axis Mundi of biological life, it was chosen by [[The Queen Commander]] and the imperial vanguard as the ultimate defensive bulwark against [[The Heavenly Demon]] and his invading armada.

## 2. Key Historical Events
* **The Non-Computable Game**: At the valley floor, mortal children and an ancient automaton played rock-paper-scissors, establishing the foundational principle of biological Qi as non-computable free will against deterministic AI prediction.
* **The Orbital Plunge**: [[Chung Jin]] entered the atmosphere at seventeen thousand miles per hour, striking the valley floor at the foot of Kailash. He redirected ninety-nine percent of the kinetic force into the subterranean bedrock, vitrifying a forty-pace crater while leaving a single pink mountain flower untouched.
* **The Trishula Strike**: It was upon these frozen slopes that [[Elder Brother]] wielded [[The Trishula]], cleanly severing the right arm of [[The Heavenly Demon]].
* **The Blackout of the Sun**: Following Kamina's death, [[Chung Jin]] ascended into the sky above Kailash to trigger [[The Solar Lotus & The Two Keys]], causing the sun to turn black as the White Lotus ignited.
