# Southfall Village (남하촌 / 南下村)
> *"Salt crust on the cedar shingles, cold grey waves beating against the sea caves, and the smell of roasting flounder over pine coals."*

Southfall Village is the starting setting of [[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]]. Though appearing to be a humble fishing settlement, it was covertly established as an experimental test ground ([[Reinforcement Learning Camps]]) by the [[Moon Sovereignty]].
