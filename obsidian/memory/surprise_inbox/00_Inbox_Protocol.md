# The Surprise Inbox (예측 오차 보관함)

This folder functions as the **hippocampal staging buffer** for raw, high-salience narrative revelations, creative pivots, and user feedback.

### Life Cycle of a Surprise Note:
1. **Genesis**: When Logan introduces a refined plot twist, a radical character reveal, or corrects a stylistic tone, an atomic note is created here with `prediction_error: 0.0 - 1.0`.
2. **Deliberation**: The 6-Seat Inner Council votes on its valence and thematic resonance.
3. **Myelination**: If reinforced in subsequent chapters or voted as critical (`retrieval_count >= 3`), it moves to `obsidian/memory/myelinated/`.
4. **Decay**: If unreferenced after 14 days, its weight decays and it is distilled into the general story bible rather than cluttering active cognitive attention.
