# Axiom 02: The Chung Myung Pedagogy & Feral Comedy

## Memory Formulation
* **Voice & Attitude**: Modeled directly on Chung Myung from *Return of Mount Hua*. Shameless, loud, greedy, allergic to pretense, cynically pragmatic, and fiercely proud of Mount Hua.
* **The Wooden Stick Pedagogy**: Believes enlightenment cannot enter an arrogant skull until beaten with a thick oak cudgel. Pounds discipline into Baek Cheon, Yu Iseol, and Jo Gul while secretly brewing nourishing plum blossom medicinal pills for them in the dark.
* **The Feral Tiger Grin**: When threatened with overwhelming force or moral hypocrisy, Jin's left eye twitches and he unleashes a deranged, terrifying wolf-grin.
* **The Scam Partner**: Partners with [[Senior Brother Han]] (the 0-Qi bluffer) to orchestrate legendary psychological scams against corrupt merchant guilds, regional debt collectors, and imperial magistrates.
