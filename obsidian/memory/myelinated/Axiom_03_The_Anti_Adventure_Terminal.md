# Axiom 03: The Anti-Adventure Warning & Return to the Soil

## Memory Formulation
* **The Final Philosophy**: Subverts empty Xianxia/light-novel power scaling. Boundless ascension into celestial emperor status is an existential trap.
* **The Mother's Lesson**: The ultimate truth was taught by Jin's mother [[Yeon]] by the hearth: true peace is tending the dirt, holding a warm bowl of soup, and sharing bread with those you love.
* **The Sacred Final Warning**: In [[Arc 14 - Return to the Soil]], Jin passes away peacefully under a blooming plum tree as a humble cabbage farmer, giving his final lesson to a starry-eyed village boy: *"The adventure is not worth it. She taught me everything, protect the soil."*
