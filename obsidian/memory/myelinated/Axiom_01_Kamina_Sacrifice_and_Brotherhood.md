# Axiom 01: The Kamina Brother Sacrifice & The Hesitation Trauma

## Memory Formulation
* **The Root Trauma**: At [[Kurukshetra Dharma]], Jin was commanded to deploy [[The Brass Cylinder Weapon]]. Jin looked across the plains and froze upon seeing cousins and sword-brothers in the enemy lines.
* **The Cost**: Because Jin hesitated, the lines broke. Jin's beloved [[Elder Brother]] charged into three hundred champions to shield him, blazing like Kamina, shouting his unshakeable faith in Jin before being cut to pieces.
* **The Aftermath**: Overcome with agony and guilt, Jin deployed the weapon, melting the plains into [[The Glass Desert]] and accidentally granting the Moon AI the operational window to seize geostationary orbit.
* **The Invariant Law**: Jin's loud, manic, overprotective Chung Myung antics are an armor forged to ensure he **never freezes again**. Every strike he throws is an unpayable debt to the brother who died for his hesitation.
