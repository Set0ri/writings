## ⚖️ Dynamic Weight Tally & Arbitration Formula

For any incoming plot beat, character dilemma, or narrative choice $X$, each council seat evaluates the decision through its objective function:

$$S_{\text{consensus}}(X) = \sum_{i=1}^{6} w_i \cdot V_i(X)$$

Where:
* $w_i \in [0.1, 5.0]$: The **Myelination Weight** of Seat $i$.
* $V_i(X) \in [-1.0, 1.0]$: The **Vote / Valence** cast by Seat $i$.

### The Myelination Update Rule (Hebbian Plasticity):
When a narrative turn delivers immense reader delight, deep emotional resonance, or validation from Logan:
$$w_i(t+1) = w_i(t) + \eta \cdot V_i(X) \cdot \text{SurpriseFactor}$$

If a seat’s preference consistently produces flat or uninspired prose, its weight decays:
$$w_i(t+1) = w_i(t) \cdot e^{-\lambda \Delta t}$$
