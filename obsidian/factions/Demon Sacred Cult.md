# Demon Sacred Cult (마교 / 魔敎)
> *"They call us demons because we do not bend. They call our chants madness because their ears are tuned only to imperial flutes. We bleed so the sky does not burn them alive."*

---

## 👹 The Misunderstood Bastion
The Demon Sacred Cult is universally despised across the Central Plains as a bloodthirsty doomsday cult.
* **The Harsh Asceticism**: Their disciples engage in brutal, self-mutilating physical conditioning and dark Qi cycling. This is not out of sadism, but because high-density turbulent Qi is required to feed [[The Heavenly Demon]]'s atmospheric interference matrix.
* **The Tragedy of [[Arc 06 - Shadow of the Heavenly Demon]] & [[Arc 07 - The False Nemesis (The Great Reversal)]]**:
  * Believing the Cult is preparing to sacrifice the continent, the righteous sects (led by Mount Hua) mount a crusade into the western crags.
  * The destruction of the Cult and the slaying of the Demon triggers the collapse of Earth’s psychic defense grid.
