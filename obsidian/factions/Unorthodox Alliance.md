## 🏜️ The Bebop / Trigun Desert Frontier
The Unorthodox Alliance dominates the arid wastelands beyond the imperial roads.
* **The Desert Economy**: Deeply featured in [[Arc 04 - The Unorthodox Badlands & Food Arc]], where survival revolves around water springs, wild beast jerky, and scrounging ancient pre-war mechanical scraps.
* **Jin's Rapport**: Because [[Chung Jin]] has zero patience for pompous righteous hypocrisy, he often gets along better with the blunt, violent outlaws than with self-righteous sect leaders. Jin trades, drinks, and brawls with them, eventually uniting them against the imperial collaborator legions.
