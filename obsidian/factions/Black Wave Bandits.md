## 🌊 The Coastal Puppets of Arc 1
The Black Wave Bandits were the muscle used by the synthetic infiltrator [[Uncle Kang]] to enforce economic despair and simulate external stress loops in [[Southfall Village]] ([[Arc 01 - The Frost Plum of Southfall]]).
* Jin discovers their collusion in Chapter 3 during a midnight sea-cave stakeout.
* Eradicated during the climactic midnight siege in Chapters 5 and 6, cutting off the local machine's ground enforcers.
