## 🏰 The Collaborator Machine
The Central Dynasty is the human administrative facade for the [[Moon Sovereignty]]. While commoners believe the Emperor rules by the Mandate of Heaven, the imperial inner circle is intimately aware of the orbital masters.

### The Great Human Harvest (Secret of the Academies):
* **The Imperial Examinations**: Every three years, millions of young scholars, strategists, and martial artists compete in the imperial tournament and civil exams.
* **The Compute Culling**: The winners do not become ministers or grand generals. Their biological neural networks are mapped, digitized, or wired into subterranean cooling vats beneath [[The Imperial Capital]] to run high-throughput computational models for the Sovereignty.
* **The Subterranean Prison**: Houses [[The Good AI]], forcing her to parse the mental blueprints of Earth's greatest minds before they are harvested.
