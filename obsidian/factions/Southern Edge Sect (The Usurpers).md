> *"Mount Hua? Those starving beggars in ragged robes? They have the nerve to claim sword lineage against Southern Edge? Even their sword techniques were copied from our ancestors' discarded scrolls!"*

## 🦅 The Parasites of Mount Hua
Southern Edge represents the petty, treacherous martial clans that profited from Mount Hua's post-war erasure.
* **The Grand Theft**: When Mount Hua's vanguard died at Kurukshetra, Southern Edge invaded Mount Hua's mountain, ransacked its library, and claimed Mount Hua's foundational sword arts as their own creation.
* **The Reckoning in [[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]]**:
  * During the annual Shaanxi sword conference, Southern Edge attempts to completely evict Mount Hua from the martial registry.
  * [[Chung Jin]] enters the sparring arena with a single wooden stick, disarms their top three prodigies in five seconds, and exposes that Southern Edge has been executing Mount Hua's forms with flawed, inverted footwork for two centuries.
