# Mount Hua Plum Blossom Sect (화산파 / 華山派)
> *"They took our ancestral halls. They stole our secret manuals and sold our jade tablets. They called us evil heretics because we refused to kneel to their corrupt court. Let them laugh. When our plum blossoms carpet this entire sky, the world will remember who taught the mountains how to hold steel."*

---

## 🌸 History & Tragedy
Two hundred years ago, Mount Hua was the undisputed sword vanguard of the Central Plains. During the Great War at [[Kurukshetra Dharma]], Mount Hua disciples formed the suicide vanguard that held the line.
* **The Slander & Erasure**: After the war, the collaborating oligarchs who allied with the Moon AI rewrote terrestrial history. Mount Hua was labeled a "traitorous heretical cult" that provoked cosmic destruction.
* **The 200-Year Decay**: Systematically starved of trade, robbed of its core sword manuals by the [[Southern Edge Sect (The Usurpers)]], and crushed by usurious loans, Mount Hua was reduced to twenty starving disciples living in leaking, drafty halls on [[Mount Hua (Hwasan)]].

## ⚡ The Rebirth Under Chung Jin
When [[Chung Jin]] arrives in [[Arc 02 - The Mud & The Blossom]]:
1. **The Stick of Re-Education**: Jin sweeps away aristocratic complacency with merciless physical training.
2. **Economic Warfare**: Uses his ancient knowledge of merchant contracts, hidden vaults, and unhinged extortion to recover stolen land and flood Mount Hua with silver.
3. **The True Plum Blossom Revival**: Teaches the genuine [[Plum Blossom Sword Technique (Mae-hwa Geom)]], restoring the impossible non-computable resonance of sword-qi.
4. **The Unifying Flame**: Mount Hua becomes the unshakeable bastion of free humanity, leading the cosmic assault against the Moon.
